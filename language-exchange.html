Starting a Language Exchange

开始语言交换

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Lena 和 Marco 在第一次语言交换见面时商量练习方式，并决定下次带照片聊天。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，内容和口语题均为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Lena · 01

Is this seat free for the language exchange?

语言交换活动这里的座位有人坐吗？

Marco · 02

It is. I am Marco. Are you here to practice Spanish?

是的。我叫 Marco。你是来练习西班牙语的吗？

Lena · 03

Yes, I'm Lena. I can help you practice English, too.

是的，我叫 Lena。我也可以帮你练习英语。

Marco · 04

That would be great. How should we divide our time?

那太好了。我们怎么分配时间呢？

Lena · 05

How about fifteen minutes in each language?

每种语言各练十五分钟怎么样？

Marco · 06

That sounds fair. Please speak slowly when we use Spanish.

这样很公平。我们说西班牙语时请说慢一点。

Lena · 07

Of course. And please tell me if I make a mistake.

当然。如果我说错了，也请告诉我。

Marco · 08

I will. Would you rather talk about travel or hobbies today?

好的。今天你更想聊旅行还是爱好？

Lena · 09

Let's start with hobbies. I like drawing after work.

先聊爱好吧。我喜欢下班后画画。

Marco · 10

I play soccer on weekends. We could bring photos next time.

我周末踢足球。下次我们可以带照片来。

Lena · 11

Good idea. Pictures will give us more to talk about.

好主意。照片会让我们有更多话题。

Marco · 12

Then let's meet here again next Thursday after work.

那我们下周四下班后再在这里见面吧。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① divide our time

分配时间，适合商量活动安排。

例句：

We can divide our time between reading and speaking.

② How about...?

提出建议：……怎么样？

例句：

How about meeting after lunch?

③ speak slowly

说慢一点，方便对方听懂。

例句：

Could you speak slowly during the call?

④ make a mistake

犯错，指语言或其他活动中的错误。

例句：

It's fine to make a mistake while learning.

⑤ start with

从某事开始。

例句：

Let's start with a short question.

03 · American Pronunciation

exchange 重音在第二音节；在 speak slowly 中，两个词开头的 /s/ 都要清楚，slowly 的重音在第一音节。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Learning languages

How do you practice a new language?

I practice with a friend once a week. We ask each other simple questions, and I write down useful new words.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A conversation partner

Describe someone you would like to practice a language with. Say who the person is, what you would talk about, and why.

I would like to practice Spanish with my friend Ana. She is patient and enjoys talking about daily life. We could meet at a cafe after work and spend half an hour speaking together. I would ask her about her favorite places and tell her about my hobbies. If I made a mistake, I would ask her to help me correct it.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Learning together

How can conversation partners help language learners?

They give learners a chance to use words in real conversations. Partners can also explain mistakes gently and help each other stay motivated.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读双方商量练习时间的句子，再邀请一位语伴并提出话题。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

Would you like to practice together? We can spend fifteen minutes in each language. Let's talk about our hobbies first.
