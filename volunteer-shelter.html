SPEAKING LESSON · 38

Volunteering at an Animal Shelter

在动物收容所做志愿者

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：1 个。

新志愿者 Hana 向动物收容所工作人员 Luis 了解当天的任务和指导安排。 本课为 2026-11-22 预制系列原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Hana · 01

This is my first day volunteering here. Where should I start?

这是我第一天在这里做志愿者。我应该从哪里开始？

Luis · 02

Welcome, Hana. Today we need help folding clean towels for the cats.

欢迎你，Hana。今天我们需要人帮猫咪叠干净的毛巾。

Hana · 03

I can do that. Where should I put them?

我可以做。叠好放在哪里？

Luis · 04

Please stack them on the shelves in the supply room.

请把它们叠放在储物间的架子上。

Hana · 05

I would also love to spend time with the animals.

我也很想和动物们待一会儿。

Luis · 06

After the towels, I can introduce you to a calm cat named Pepper.

叠完毛巾后，我可以介绍你认识一只叫 Pepper 的温顺猫咪。

Hana · 07

That sounds lovely. Is there anything I should know first?

太好了。我需要先了解什么吗？

Luis · 08

I will show you how our staff approach her and stay with you.

我会示范我们工作人员怎样接近她，并陪着你。

Hana · 09

Thank you. I do not want to make her nervous.

谢谢。我不想让她紧张。

Luis · 10

That is thoughtful. We let each animal set the pace.

你想得很周到。我们让每只动物按自己的节奏来。

Hana · 11

I will start with the towels while you finish your work.

那我先叠毛巾，你继续忙。

Luis · 12

Great. I will meet you in the supply room shortly.

好。我一会儿到储物间找你。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① volunteer

做志愿者，无偿帮忙。

例句：

I volunteer at a local food pantry.

② supply room

存放用品的房间。

例句：

The extra paper is in the supply room.

③ spend time with

花时间陪伴。

例句：

She likes to spend time with her grandparents.

④ make someone nervous

让某人紧张。

例句：

A loud noise can make the dog nervous.

⑤ set the pace

决定进行的节奏。

例句：

Let the beginner set the pace of the walk.

03 · American Pronunciation

volunteer 重音在最后一个音节；shelter 重音在第一音节，结尾的 r 用美式卷舌音。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Helping

Have you ever helped an organization in your community?

Yes. I helped a local shelter sort clean towels. It was a small task, but it gave the staff more time for other work.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A volunteer experience

Describe a volunteer activity you would like to try. Say where you would go, what you would do, and why.

I would like to volunteer at an animal shelter near my home. On my first visit, I could help fold clean towels and organize supplies. I would also ask a staff member to show me how to spend time with a calm cat. I would follow their guidance and let the animal move at its own pace. I think the work would be useful and rewarding.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Volunteering

Why do organizations need volunteers for both simple and skilled tasks?

Simple tasks still take time, so volunteers can help staff focus on work that needs special training. Clear guidance also helps everyone understand their role.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读第一天询问任务的对话，再说明你愿意做什么以及需要什么指导。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

Where should I start? I can fold the clean towels. Could you show me where to put them?
