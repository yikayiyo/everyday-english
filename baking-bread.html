SPEAKING LESSON · 29

Baking Bread Together

一起烤面包

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Clara 和弟弟 Max 在家尝试做一条面包，商量等待发酵和烘烤的步骤。本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Clara · 01

The dough feels sticky. Should we add more flour?

面团摸起来很黏。我们要再加面粉吗？

Max · 02

Let's add a little at a time and mix it in.

我们一次加一点，搅拌进去吧。

Clara · 03

That feels better. What does the recipe say next?

感觉好多了。食谱接下来怎么说？

Max · 04

Cover the bowl and let the dough rise until it gets bigger.

盖上碗，让面团发酵膨胀。

Clara · 05

Can we leave it on the kitchen counter?

我们可以把它放在厨房台面上吗？

Max · 06

Yes, the recipe suggests a warm spot. This corner should work.

可以，食谱建议放在温暖的地方。这个角落应该合适。

Clara · 07

It's much bigger now. Should I shape it into a loaf?

现在大多了。我把它整形成一条面包吗？

Max · 08

Please do. I'll get the pan ready while you do that.

麻烦你了。我趁你整形时把烤盘准备好。

Clara · 09

The dough is in the pan. How long should it bake?

面团放进烤盘了。要烤多久？

Max · 10

Our recipe says about thirty minutes. Let's set a timer and check it then.

我们的食谱说大约三十分钟。我们定个闹钟，到时检查一下。

Clara · 11

The top looks golden now, and it smells wonderful.

现在表面看起来金黄，闻起来真香。

Max · 12

Let's let it cool before we slice it. I can't wait to try some.

我们切之前先让它冷却。我迫不及待想尝尝了。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① a little at a time

一次少量，逐步进行。

例句：

Add the milk a little at a time.

② let the dough rise

让面团发酵膨胀。

例句：

Let the dough rise in the covered bowl.

③ get the pan ready

准备好烤盘或锅。

例句：

I'll get the pan ready for the vegetables.

④ set a timer

设置计时器。

例句：

Set a timer for ten minutes.

⑤ can't wait to

迫不及待想做某事。

例句：

I can't wait to see your new place.

03 · American Pronunciation

dough 和 though 韵尾发相同的长元音 /oʊ/，gh 不发音；loaf 结尾的 f 清晰送气。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Baking

Do you enjoy baking?

Yes, although I do not bake often. I like the smell of fresh bread and the calm feeling of working in the kitchen.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Making food with someone

Describe a time you made food with another person. Say what you made, what each of you did, and how it turned out.

I made a loaf of bread with my brother on a rainy afternoon. We followed a recipe and mixed the flour and other ingredients in a large bowl. I shaped the dough while he prepared the pan. We waited for it to bake and watched the top turn golden. After it cooled, we each had a slice. It was a little uneven, but it tasted good, and we enjoyed making it together.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Cooking together

Why might people enjoy making food together?

They can share the work and learn from each other. Preparing food also gives them time to talk, and eating the result can make the experience feel rewarding.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练
