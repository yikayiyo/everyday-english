SPEAKING LESSON · 53

Checking Ingredients at a Potluck

聚餐时确认食物成分

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：1 个。

Nina 在朋友 Sam 的聚餐中对花生过敏；两人查看包装并在无法确认食物成分时选择不吃。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目是原创口语练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Nina · 01

Everything looks lovely, Sam. Could I ask about the snacks?

Sam，这些食物看起来真不错。我能问问零食的事吗？

Sam · 02

Sure. What would you like to know?

当然。你想了解什么？

Nina · 03

I have a peanut allergy. Do you still have the package for these crackers?

我对花生过敏。你还留着这些饼干的包装吗？

Sam · 04

Yes, it's in the kitchen. Let's read the label together.

有，在厨房。我们一起看看标签吧。

Nina · 05

Thanks. I want to check the ingredients and any allergy information.

谢谢。我想看看配料和过敏原相关信息。

Sam · 06

Here's the package. Take your time.

包装在这儿。你慢慢看。

Nina · 07

The crackers aren't a choice I'm comfortable with, so I'll skip them.

这些饼干让我不太放心，我就不吃了。

Sam · 08

Of course. I also brought some fruit in its original container.

当然可以。我还带了一些水果，装在原来的容器里。

Nina · 09

May I check that label too?

我可以也看看那个标签吗？

Sam · 10

Absolutely. I don't know what's in the homemade dip, though.

当然可以。不过我不知道自制蘸酱里有什么。

Nina · 11

Then I'll pass on the dip. I'll have the fruit after checking it.

那我就不吃蘸酱了。检查过以后我吃水果。

Sam · 12

Sounds good. Thanks for telling me what you need.

好。谢谢你告诉我你的需求。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① have an allergy

对某物过敏；可接 to 加具体物质。

例句：

I have an allergy to peanuts.

② read the label

阅读包装标签。

例句：

Please read the label on the jar.

③ take your time

慢慢来，不必着急。

例句：

Take your time choosing a seat.

④ skip it

不吃、不参加或跳过。

例句：

I'll skip dessert today, thanks.

⑤ pass on

婉拒某物。

例句：

I'll pass on the sauce, thank you.

03 · American Pronunciation

allergy 重音在第一个音节；label 也重读第一个音节，末尾是轻读的 bəl。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Sharing food

Do you enjoy sharing food with friends?

Yes. I like trying different dishes, and I appreciate it when friends tell me what is in them.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A meal with others

Describe a meal you shared with other people. Say where it was, what you ate, and what made it memorable.

A friend invited me to a lunch where everyone brought something. I brought sliced fruit in its original container. Before eating, one guest asked about the ingredients in a snack, and we looked at the package together. She chose the fruit after checking its label and politely declined a dip with unknown ingredients. I remember the lunch because everyone listened and made her feel welcome.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Inclusive gatherings

How can hosts help guests with different food needs feel welcome?

Hosts can ask guests about their needs in advance and keep ingredient information available. They can also respect a guest's decision to avoid a dish without making them feel uncomfortable.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读后礼貌说明过敏情况，要求查看标签，并婉拒无法确认成分的食物。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

I have a peanut allergy. May I check the package? I can't confirm what's in the dip, so I'll pass, thank you.
