SPEAKING LESSON · 52

Herbs on a Sunny Windowsill

在窗台种香草

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Anna 和 Ben 为厨房窗台挑选盆栽香草，商量光照和浇水安排。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目是原创口语练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Anna · 01

I'd like to grow some herbs on our kitchen windowsill.

我想在厨房窗台种一些香草。

Ben · 02

That's a nice idea. Which ones do you use most?

这主意不错。你最常用哪些？

Anna · 03

Basil and mint, but I only have room for two small pots.

罗勒和薄荷，不过我只放得下两个小盆。

Ben · 04

Then let's put one plant in each pot.

那我们每个盆里种一株吧。

Anna · 05

The window gets sun in the morning. Is that enough for them?

窗户早上能晒到太阳。对它们来说够吗？

Ben · 06

Let's check the care tags before we choose the plants.

我们选植物之前先看看养护标签吧。

Anna · 07

This basil tag suggests a sunny spot. The mint tag says it can take some shade.

罗勒的标签建议放在阳光充足处。薄荷的标签说它能适应一些阴凉。

Ben · 08

We can put the basil closer to the window.

我们可以把罗勒放得更靠近窗户。

Anna · 09

These pots have holes underneath. I'll put a tray under them.

这些盆底下有孔。我会在下面放个托盘。

Ben · 10

Good plan. Let's check the soil before adding water.

好主意。浇水前我们先检查土壤。

Anna · 11

I'll check it in the morning when I open the curtains.

我早上拉开窗帘时会检查一下。

Ben · 12

And I'll check the tags again if either plant starts looking unhappy.

如果哪株植物看起来状态不好，我会再看看标签。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① have room for

有空间放下。

例句：

We have room for a small table.

② care tag

植物上的养护标签。

例句：

Read the care tag before choosing a spot.

③ sunny spot

阳光充足的地方。

例句：

The cat sleeps in a sunny spot.

④ closer to

更靠近。

例句：

Move the chair closer to the desk.

⑤ check the soil

检查土壤。

例句：

Check the soil before you water the plant.

03 · American Pronunciation

basil 在美式英语中常以第一个音节重读，首音节与 bay 同音；soil 是一个音节，注意其中的双元音。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Plants at home

Would you like to grow a plant at home?

Yes. I'd like to grow mint near my window because I enjoy its fresh smell.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A small project at home

Describe a small project you did at home. Say what you wanted to do, what you needed, and how it went.

I decided to grow herbs on my windowsill last spring. I bought a small mint plant, a pot with a hole, and a tray. First I checked the tag to find a suitable place. Then I put the pot near the window and checked the soil regularly. It was satisfying to see new leaves appear, and I enjoyed adding a few to my tea.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Learning from plants

What can people learn from taking care of plants?

Plants can remind people to observe changes and be patient. They also show that different living things may need different amounts of light and water.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读后介绍你想种的香草、摆放位置和照看方法。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

I'd like to grow mint in a small pot. I'll read the care tag and check the soil before watering it.
