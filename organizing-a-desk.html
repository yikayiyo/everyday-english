Making Space on a Crowded Desk

整理拥挤的书桌

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Clara 收拾堆满纸张的书桌，室友 Jamie 帮她区分需要保留和处理的物品。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，内容和练习题均为原创，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Clara · 01

I can't find my notebook under all these papers.

这么多纸堆在一起，我找不到笔记本了。

Jamie · 02

Let's clear a little space first. Are these receipts still useful?

我们先腾出一点地方吧。这些收据还有用吗？

Clara · 03

Some are. I'll check the dates before I throw any away.

有些有用。我扔之前会先看看日期。

Jamie · 04

I'll put the pens in this cup while you sort them.

你整理收据的时候，我把笔放进这个杯子里。

Clara · 05

Thanks. The notebook was under that stack of mail!

谢谢。笔记本原来在那一叠邮件下面！

Jamie · 06

Would a tray help you keep new mail together?

用个托盘把新邮件放在一起会有帮助吗？

Clara · 07

Yes. I can put the tray on the left side of the desk.

会。我可以把托盘放在桌子左边。

Jamie · 08

What about the charger that's stretched across the table?

那根横在桌上的充电线怎么办？

Clara · 09

I'll unplug it and coil it up when I'm not using it.

不用的时候我会拔掉插头，把它绕好。

Jamie · 10

There's enough room for your notebook now.

现在有足够的空间放你的笔记本了。

Clara · 11

Much better. I'll spend a few minutes sorting the desk each Friday.

好多了。我以后每周五花几分钟整理桌面。

Jamie · 12

That sounds doable. Now you can start your work.

听起来可以做到。现在你可以开始工作了。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① clear space

腾出空间。

例句：

Let's clear some space for the new shelf.

② throw away

扔掉。

例句：

Don't throw away that envelope yet.

③ sort

分类整理。

例句：

I'll sort the photos by date.

④ a stack of

一叠……。

例句：

There's a stack of magazines by the sofa.

⑤ coil up

把线等盘绕起来。

例句：

Coil up the cable and put it in the drawer.

03 · American Pronunciation

desk 末尾 /sk/ 都要发出；charger 重读第一音节，开头和中间的字母组合都含 /dʒ/ 音。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Workspaces

Do you prefer a tidy desk?

Yes. I find it easier to focus when I can find my notebook and pens. I try to clear my desk at the end of the day.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Organizing a space

Describe a space you organized. Say what it was like before, what you did, and how you felt afterward.

My desk was covered with papers, and I kept losing my notebook. One weekend my roommate helped me make space. I checked some old receipts while he put the pens in a cup. We found my notebook under a stack of mail and placed a tray on the desk for new letters. Afterward I could see the surface again, and starting my work felt much easier.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Organization

How can an organized workspace affect the way people work?

It can save time because useful things are easier to find. However, people organize differently, so a system should fit the work they actually do.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读整理过程，再说明你如何处理桌上的纸张和充电线。

08 · 今日口头回答练习
