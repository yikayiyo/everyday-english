SPEAKING LESSON · 10

Seeing a Doctor at a Clinic

在诊所看医生

A2 → B115–20 分钟诊所 · 看病

Maya 因为从周二开始喉咙痛，走进诊所看医生；她向 Owen 医生说明症状、有没有发烧或咳嗽、药物过敏，并询问用药方法和是否需要复诊。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Maya · 01

Hi. I'd like to see a doctor about a sore throat. It started on Tuesday.

你好。我想看医生，因为喉咙痛。从周二就开始了。

Owen · 02

Sure. Besides the sore throat, have you noticed any other symptoms?

没问题。除了喉咙痛，你还注意到其他症状吗？

Maya · 03

Yes, I also have a headache, and it's a little hard to swallow.

有的，我还有点头疼，而且吞咽有点困难。

Owen · 04

I see. Have you had a fever or a cough?

明白了。你有发烧或咳嗽吗？

Maya · 05

I felt a bit warm last night, but I don't have a cough.

昨晚我觉得有点发热，但没有咳嗽。

Owen · 06

Okay. Are you allergic to any medicine?

好的。你对什么药物过敏吗？

Maya · 07

Not that I know of. I haven't had any problems before.

据我所知没有。以前也没出过什么问题。

Owen · 08

Good. I'll prescribe some lozenges and a short course of medicine.

很好。我会给你开一些含片和一个短期疗程的药。

Maya · 09

Thank you. How often should I take the medicine?

谢谢。这个药应该多久吃一次？

Owen · 10

Take it twice a day after meals for five days.

每天两次，饭后服用，连吃五天。

Maya · 11

Got it. Should I come back if it doesn't get better?

明白了。如果没有好转，我需要再来复诊吗？

Owen · 12

Yes. If it lasts more than a week, come in again.

需要。如果持续一周以上，请再来一趟。

听力理解

不看中文，再听一遍对话，尝试回答：

What symptoms does Maya have, and how should she take the medicine?

Maya 有哪些症状？药应该怎么吃？

查看答案

喉咙痛从周二开始，还有头痛、吞咽有点困难；昨晚有点发热，但没有咳嗽。她没有已知的药物过敏。医生开了含片和一个短期疗程的药，每天两次、饭后服用、连吃五天；如果持续一周以上还没好转，需要复诊。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① See a doctor about

因某症状去看医生。说明看病原因时常用。

例句：

I'd like to see a doctor about a sore throat.

② Notice any other symptoms

注意到其他症状。医生询问病情时常用。

例句：

Besides the sore throat, have you noticed any other symptoms?

③ Allergic to any medicine

对药物过敏。开药前医生常会确认。

例句：

Are you allergic to any medicine?

④ Prescribe a short course of medicine

开一个短期疗程的药。医生说明用药安排时常用。

例句：

I'll prescribe some lozenges and a short course of medicine.

⑤ Come back if it doesn't get better

如果没有好转就再来复诊。询问或建议复诊时常用。

例句：

Should I come back if it doesn't get better?

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 sore throat 的节奏：SORE THROAT。注意 swallow 的重音在前：SWALlow，allergic 重音在第二音节：a-LLER-gic，以及 prescribe 的重音在后：pre-SCRIBE。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。说症状时突出 SORE throat，问过敏时突出 a-LLER-gic，说用法时突出 TWICE a day。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Health and seeing a doctor

Do you usually see a doctor when you feel unwell, or do you wait and rest at home?

参考回答（入门训练版）

I usually rest at home if I just have a mild cold. But if I have a sore throat or a fever for several days, I see a doctor at a clinic.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A time you visited a clinic

Describe a time you went to see a doctor. Say why you went, what the doctor asked you, and what happened afterwards.

参考回答（入门训练版）

Last winter I went to a clinic because of a sore throat. The doctor asked about my symptoms and medicine allergies, then prescribed lozenges and a short course of medicine. I felt better after a few days.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Health care in daily life

Why do some people delay seeing a doctor even when they feel unwell?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Some people think the illness will go away by itself, or they are too busy to visit a clinic. Others may worry about the cost or about taking medicine. However, seeing a doctor early can prevent a small problem from becoming worse.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习说明自己的症状、有没有药物过敏，以及如何按时吃药和是否需要复诊。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意症状描述、药物过敏、用药剂量和复诊建议。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ①、③、⑥、⑩、⑫ 句的症状、过敏、剂量和复诊表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Maya 和 Owen，把症状、过敏情况和用药次数换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你会怎么在诊所看病：症状、药物过敏、用药方法和是否需要复诊。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

What symptoms would you describe to the doctor?

Are you allergic to any medicine?

How often should you take the medicine, and for how many days?

When would you come back if it doesn't get better?
