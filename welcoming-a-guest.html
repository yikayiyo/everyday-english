SPEAKING LESSON · 54

Making a Guest Feel at Home

欢迎客人来家里

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：2 个。

Tara 的朋友 Alex 首次来她家过夜；她介绍房间并安排早餐。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目是原创口语练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Tara · 01

Welcome, Alex! Come in and put your bag down.

欢迎你，Alex！进来把包放下吧。

Alex · 02

Thanks, Tara. It's good to see you after such a long trip.

谢谢，Tara。走了这么远的路，见到你真好。

Tara · 03

You must be tired. Would you like some water first?

你一定累了。要先喝点水吗？

Alex · 04

Yes, please. Then could you show me where I can leave my bag?

好，谢谢。然后能告诉我把包放在哪里吗？

Tara · 05

Your room is at the end of the hall, next to the bathroom.

你的房间在走廊尽头，浴室旁边。

Alex · 06

That's easy to find. Is there a towel I can use?

很容易找到。有我可以用的毛巾吗？

Tara · 07

I've put a clean one on the bed, along with an extra blanket.

我在床上放了一条干净的毛巾，还有一条备用毯子。

Alex · 08

Perfect. What time do you usually have breakfast?

太好了。你通常几点吃早餐？

Tara · 09

Around eight, but there's no need to get up early for me.

大约八点，不过你不用为了我早起。

Alex · 10

I might sleep a little longer, if that's all right.

如果可以的话，我可能会多睡一会儿。

Tara · 11

Of course. Help yourself to breakfast whenever you're ready.

当然。你准备好后随时自己拿早餐吃。

Alex · 12

Thank you. I'll settle in and join you in a little while.

谢谢。我先安顿一下，过一会儿来找你。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① put your bag down

把包放下。

例句：

Put your bag down beside the chair.

② at the end of

在……尽头。

例句：

The elevator is at the end of the hall.

③ along with

连同、还有。

例句：

I packed a hat along with my coat.

④ help yourself to

请自取、随意享用。

例句：

Help yourself to some tea.

⑤ settle in

安顿下来，适应新地方。

例句：

It took me a day to settle in.

03 · American Pronunciation

towel 通常有两个音节，重音在第一音节；在 help yourself 中注意 help 末尾的 /p/。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Guests

Do you like having guests stay at your home?

Yes, when I have enough space. I enjoy talking over breakfast and showing them around my neighborhood.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A visit

Describe a time you visited someone and felt welcome. Say who you visited, what they did, and how you felt.

I once stayed with a friend in another city for a weekend. When I arrived, she offered me water and showed me the room where I could sleep. She had put a clean towel on the bed and told me I could have breakfast whenever I was ready. Her small acts of kindness helped me relax after my journey. I felt comfortable asking questions.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Hospitality

Why do small acts of hospitality matter to visitors?

Visitors may feel tired or uncertain in an unfamiliar place. Clear information and a warm welcome can help them relax and feel included.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读后欢迎一位留宿朋友，说明房间、毛巾和早餐安排。

08 · 今日口头回答练习
