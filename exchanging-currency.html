Exchanging Currency

在兑换处换汇

A2 → B115–20 分钟旅行 · 换汇

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听；尚未进行浏览器播放或卡拉OK交互验证。第 6、8、10、12 句共有 5 个零时长词（of、count、receipt、the、receipt），可能无法高亮。

Maya 准备去日本旅行，在兑换处把美元换成日元；店员 Jordan 说明当天汇率和手续费，核验护照并完成兑换。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Maya · 01

Hi, I'd like to exchange some US dollars for Japanese yen, please.

你好，我想把一些美元换成日元。

Jordan · 02

Of course. How much would you like to exchange today?

当然可以。你今天想换多少？

Maya · 03

Three hundred dollars. What's the rate today?

三百美元。今天的汇率是多少？

Jordan · 04

It's 148 yen to the dollar. There's also a small service fee.

一美元兑 148 日元。另外还要收一笔小额手续费。

Maya · 05

How much is the fee?

手续费是多少？

Jordan · 06

Five dollars. We can take it from the dollars or out of the yen. Either way is fine.

五美元。我们可以从美元里扣，也可以从日元里扣。两种都可以。

Maya · 07

Please take it out of the yen. Do you need to see my ID?

请从日元里扣吧。需要看我的证件吗？

Jordan · 08

Yes, a passport or a driver's license is fine. I'll also need to count the cash.

需要，护照或驾照都可以。我还需要清点一下现金。

Maya · 09

Sure. Here's my passport and the three hundred dollars.

好的。这是我的护照，还有这三百美元。

Jordan · 10

Thanks. Everything looks good. Please sign the receipt here.

谢谢。都没问题。请在这张回单上签字。

Maya · 11

Got it. Will I get a better rate if I come back later?

好的。如果我晚一点再来，汇率会更好吗？

Jordan · 12

It might change, but not by much today. Here's your yen and your copy of the receipt. Have a good trip!

可能会变，但今天幅度不会太大。这是你的日元，还有回单副本。祝你旅途愉快！

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① I'd like to exchange some US dollars for Japanese yen

想把美元换成日元，在兑换处说明兑换方向时常用。

例句：

Hi, I'd like to exchange some US dollars for Japanese yen, please.

② What's the rate today?

今天的汇率是多少？询问当天牌价的常用问法。

例句：

Three hundred dollars. What's the rate today?

③ service fee

手续费。兑换处常会另外收取一小笔费用。

例句：

There's also a small service fee.

④ take it out of the yen

从日元里扣除（手续费），用来选择扣费方式。

例句：

Please take it out of the yen.

⑤ Have a good trip!

祝你旅途愉快！在顾客办完兑换、准备出发时常用的道别。

例句：

Here's your yen and your copy of the receipt. Have a good trip!

03 · American Pronunciation

exchange 读作 /ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ/，重音在第二音节；yen 读作 /jen/；receipt 的 p 不发音，读作 /rɪˈsiːt/；license 读作 /ˈlaɪsəns/；dollar 的重音在第一音节 /ˈdɑːlər/。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Travel money

Do you usually exchange money before you travel?

Yes, I do. I like to exchange a little cash before a trip, so I can pay for a taxi or a snack when I arrive. I usually keep the rest of my money on a card.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Exchanging money

Describe an imagined time you exchanged money for a trip. Say where you were, what currency you needed, and how you asked about the rate and the fee.

Imagine that I am at a currency exchange desk because I am going to Japan. I tell the clerk I would like to exchange three hundred US dollars for Japanese yen, and I ask about the rate today. The clerk explains the rate and a small service fee, and I ask them to take the fee out of the yen. I show my passport, sign the receipt, and take the cash for my trip.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Cash and cards

Why do some travelers prefer to use cards instead of cash?

Cards can be safer to carry, and you do not have to exchange a large amount of cash in advance. They also make it easier to keep a record of spending. Still, a little cash is useful when a shop or a taxi does not take cards.

08 · 今日口头回答练习
