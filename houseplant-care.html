Caring for a Houseplant

照顾室内植物

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Ella 请邻居 Ben 在她出门期间照看一盆室内植物。本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Ella · 01

Would you mind looking after my plant while I'm away?

我不在的时候，你介意帮我照看一下植物吗？

Ben · 02

Of course not. How long will you be gone?

当然不介意。你要离开多久？

Ella · 03

Just a week. It's the plant on the living room shelf.

只有一周。就是客厅架子上的那盆植物。

Ben · 04

Does it need a lot of water?

它需要很多水吗？

Ella · 05

Not much. I watered it today, so please check the soil next Wednesday.

不需要太多。我今天浇过了，所以下周三请检查一下土。

Ben · 06

What should I do if the soil feels dry?

如果土摸起来干了，我该怎么办？

Ella · 07

Give it a little water, slowly, until the soil feels damp.

慢慢给它浇一点水，直到土摸起来湿润。

Ben · 08

Should I move it closer to the window?

我要把它移得离窗户近一点吗？

Ella · 09

Please leave it where it is. The bright light there seems to suit it.

请把它留在原处。那里的明亮光线似乎适合它。

Ben · 10

I can do that. I'll send you a picture when I check it.

没问题。我检查时给你发张照片。

Ella · 11

Thank you. I'll leave a note beside the pot to remind you.

谢谢。我会在花盆旁留张便条提醒你。

Ben · 12

Perfect. Enjoy your trip, and don't worry about the plant.

太好了。祝你旅途愉快，别担心植物。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① look after

照看、照顾。

例句：

Could you look after my cat tomorrow?

② be away

不在家或不在某地。

例句：

I'll be away for the weekend.

③ feel dry

摸起来干燥。

例句：

The towel still feels dry.

④ leave it where it is

把它留在原处。

例句：

The lamp looks good there; leave it where it is.

⑤ remind you

提醒你。

例句：

I'll remind you about the meeting.

03 · American Pronunciation

water 在美式英语中间的 t 常读成轻快的闪音；soil 是一个音节，不要拆成两个。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Plants

Do you keep plants at home?

Yes, I have one small plant by my window. I like seeing something green when I work at my desk.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Caring for something

Describe something you looked after for someone. Say what it was, what you did, and how it went.

My neighbor asked me to look after her houseplant while she visited her family. She showed me where it stood and told me when to check the soil. I went over once during the week and found the soil a little dry, so I gave the plant some water. I also sent her a photo. When she came home, she thanked me, and I was glad I could help.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Responsibility

Why is it helpful to leave clear instructions when someone helps you?

Clear instructions make it easier for the other person to know what to do. They can also prevent confusion, especially when the task is unfamiliar or the owner is away.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读后请朋友照看植物，说明检查时间、浇水方式和摆放位置。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

Could you look after my plant? Please check the soil on Wednesday and leave the pot by the window.
