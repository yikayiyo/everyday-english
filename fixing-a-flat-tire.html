Calling for Help with a Flat Tire

轮胎漏气时寻求专业帮助

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

停车场里，Rachel 发现车胎漏气；朋友 Omar 陪她联系道路救援，并等专业人员处理。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，练习题为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Rachel · 01

My car tire looks flat. I do not think I should drive on it.

我的车胎看起来瘪了。我觉得不该继续开。

Omar · 02

Let us stay here in the parking lot and call for help.

我们留在停车场这里，打电话求助吧。

Rachel · 03

I have the number for my roadside assistance service.

我有道路救援服务的电话号码。

Omar · 04

Good. Tell them where the car is and what you can see.

好。告诉他们车在哪里，以及你看到的情况。

Rachel · 05

I told them we are in the lot beside the grocery store.

我告诉他们我们在杂货店旁的停车场。

Omar · 06

Did they say when someone might arrive?

他们说了大概什么时候会有人来吗？

Rachel · 07

They expect a technician in about forty minutes, depending on traffic.

他们预计技师大约四十分钟后到，具体要看路况。

Omar · 08

We can wait inside the store if you prefer.

如果你愿意，我们可以到店里等。

Rachel · 09

Thanks. I gave them my number so they can call when they arrive.

谢谢。我把号码给了他们，他们到了可以打给我。

Omar · 10

Do you know whether they can repair it here?

你知道他们能否在这里修好吗？

Rachel · 11

They said the technician will inspect it first and explain the options.

他们说技师会先检查，再说明有哪些选择。

Omar · 12

That sounds sensible. Let us wait for the professional.

这样很妥当。我们等专业人员来吧。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① a flat tire

瘪胎、漏气的轮胎。

例句：

I noticed a flat tire in the parking lot.

② roadside assistance

道路救援服务。

例句：

She called roadside assistance for help.

③ depending on traffic

取决于路况。

例句：

The trip could take an hour, depending on traffic.

④ inspect it first

先检查它。

例句：

The mechanic will inspect it first.

⑤ explain the options

说明可选方案。

例句：

The technician will explain the options after checking.

03 · American Pronunciation

tire 在美式英语中通常读作一个音节或接近一个音节，尾部带 r 音；technician 重音在中间的 NI 音节。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Travel problems

What do you do when a journey does not go as planned?

I try to find a safe place to stop and then contact someone who can help. I also let the people waiting for me know about the delay.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Getting help

Describe a time you needed help during a journey. Say what happened, whom you contacted, and how the situation ended.

Once I noticed that a tire looked flat when I returned to my car in a parking lot. I stayed there and called my roadside assistance service. I explained my location and gave them my phone number. A technician arrived after a wait and inspected the tire. The technician explained the available options, and I arranged to have the tire dealt with professionally. I was relieved that I had asked for help.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Asking for help

Why do some people hesitate to ask for professional help?

They may worry about the cost or think they should solve every problem themselves. Clear information about the service can help them decide what support they need.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读说明位置和询问到达时间的句子，再练习向救援人员描述情况。
