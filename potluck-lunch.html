Organizing a Potluck Lunch

分工筹备自带菜午餐

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：2 个。

Molly 和同事 Dan 为团队自带菜午餐分配食物和用品，避免大家带同一种东西。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，练习题为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Molly · 01

Our team potluck is on Friday. Have you decided what to bring?

我们的团队自带菜午餐在周五。你决定带什么了吗？

Dan · 02

I was thinking of a bean salad. Is anyone else bringing one?

我在想带一份豆子沙拉。还有别人带这个吗？

Molly · 03

Let me check the list. So far we have bread and fruit.

我看看清单。目前有面包和水果。

Dan · 04

Then the salad should add something different.

那沙拉应该能增加一种不同的选择。

Molly · 05

That sounds good. I can bring a big bowl of rice.

听起来不错。我可以带一大碗米饭。

Dan · 06

Do we have serving spoons, or should I bring some?

我们有分菜勺吗，还是我带几个？

Molly · 07

Please bring two if you can. I will bring plates and napkins.

如果可以，请带两个。我来带盘子和餐巾。

Dan · 08

Sure. Where should we put everything when we arrive?

好。我们到了以后把东西放在哪里？

Molly · 09

The table by the window has space for the dishes.

窗边的桌子有地方放菜。

Dan · 10

I can get there a little early and help set it up.

我可以早一点到，帮忙布置。

Molly · 11

Thanks. I will update the list so everyone can see our plans.

谢谢。我会更新清单，让大家都看到我们的安排。

Dan · 12

Great. I am looking forward to lunch with the team.

太好了。我很期待和团队一起吃午饭。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① potluck

自带菜聚餐，参加者各带食物分享。

例句：

Our office is having a potluck next week.

② so far

到目前为止。

例句：

So far, three people have signed up.

③ add something different

增加不同的选择。

例句：

A vegetable dish would add something different.

④ serving spoon

分菜勺，用于给大家盛取食物。

例句：

Please put a serving spoon beside the rice.

⑤ set it up

把它布置好或准备好。

例句：

We can set up the table before the guests arrive.

03 · American Pronunciation

potluck 重音在第一个音节 POT；spoon 以 /sp/ 开头，注意不要在 s 前加元音。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Shared meals

Do you enjoy sharing meals with coworkers or classmates?

Yes. It gives us time to talk about things outside work, and everyone can contribute something they enjoy.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A shared meal

Describe a meal where everyone contributed something. Say who was there, what people brought, and what made it enjoyable.

My coworkers and I once arranged a shared lunch in our office. We made a list so that people could bring different dishes and supplies. I brought a bowl of rice, and another coworker brought a bean salad. Two people arrived early to arrange the table and put out plates. We tried a little of everything and talked during the break. It felt friendly and easy to organize together.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Group planning

What helps a group organize a meal successfully?

A shared list makes it easier to see what is still needed. Clear responsibilities also prevent one person from doing all the work.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读分配食物和用品的句子，再提出你愿意为聚餐带的东西。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

I can bring a salad. Is anyone bringing serving spoons? I can arrive early to help set up the table.
