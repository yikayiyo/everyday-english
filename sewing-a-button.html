SPEAKING LESSON · 43

A Button Back in Place

把纽扣缝回去

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：5 个。

Tessa 的外套纽扣掉了，朋友 Will 帮她找针线，她自己把纽扣缝回原处。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，练习题为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Tessa · 01

A button fell off my coat this morning. Do you have a needle and thread?

我外套上的一颗纽扣今天早上掉了。你有针线吗？

Will · 02

I do. Did you manage to keep the button?

有。你把纽扣留住了吗？

Tessa · 03

Yes, I put it in my pocket before it rolled away.

留住了。它滚走前我把它放进了口袋。

Will · 04

Good catch. What color thread would match the coat?

幸好你及时收好了。什么颜色的线和外套相配？

Tessa · 05

Dark blue would be close enough. The original thread was blue.

深蓝色就差不多。原来的线是蓝色的。

Will · 06

Here is my sewing kit. There is a small pair of scissors inside.

这是我的针线盒。里面有一把小剪刀。

Tessa · 07

Thanks. I fixed a button before, but that was a long time ago.

谢谢。我以前缝补过纽扣，不过那是很久以前的事了。

Will · 08

Take your time. You can sit at the table where the light is better.

慢慢来。你可以坐在桌边，那里的光线更好。

Tessa · 09

That helps. Could you hold the coat while I get started?

那太好了。我开始缝的时候，你能帮我拿着外套吗？

Will · 10

Sure. Let me know when you have it in place.

当然。放到位了告诉我一声。

Tessa · 11

There, it is secure again. I will trim the loose thread.

好了，它又缝牢了。我把多余的线头剪掉。

Will · 12

Great job. You can wear your coat tomorrow.

做得真好。明天你可以穿这件外套了。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① fall off

掉落、脱落。

例句：

A button fell off my shirt.

② roll away

滚走。

例句：

The coin rolled away under the chair.

③ close enough

足够接近，基本合适。

例句：

This shade of blue is close enough.

④ take your time

慢慢来，不必着急。

例句：

Take your time choosing a color.

⑤ in place

在正确的位置。

例句：

Hold the label in place while I tape it.

03 · American Pronunciation

thread 的 th 是清辅音 /θ/，舌尖轻触上下齿之间；sewn 与 so 韵母相同，末尾有 /n/。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Clothes

Do you prefer repairing clothes or replacing them?

I try to repair small problems first. If a button comes off, sewing it back is usually easier than finding a new coat.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Fixing something

Describe a small thing you repaired. Say what was wrong, how you fixed it, and how you felt.

A button came off my favorite coat just before a trip. I found the button in my bag and borrowed a sewing kit from a friend. I chose a similar color of thread and sat near a bright window. The job took me a little while because I had not sewn recently. When the button was secure, I felt pleased that I could wear the coat again.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Repairing items

Why do some people replace things instead of repairing them?

They may lack the time, tools, or skills to repair an item. Easy access to repair services and clear advice could make fixing things more convenient.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读借针线及请求帮忙的句子，然后说明一件衣服出了什么问题。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

A button fell off my shirt. Could I borrow your sewing kit? I have the button, and blue thread would match.
