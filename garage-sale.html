SPEAKING LESSON · 61

Finding a Lamp at a Garage Sale

在车库旧货售卖中挑选台灯

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Tessa 在邻居 Daniel 的车库旧货售卖中寻找阅读灯，两人商量试灯和价格。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，内容和练习题均为原创，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Tessa · 01

Good morning! Is this lamp still for sale?

早上好！这盏灯还卖吗？

Daniel · 02

Yes, it is. I used it on my desk for years.

卖。我在书桌上用了它好多年。

Tessa · 03

I'm looking for a reading lamp. May I turn it on?

我正在找一盏阅读灯。我可以打开试试吗？

Daniel · 04

Sure. There's an outlet next to the garage door.

当然。车库门旁边有个插座。

Tessa · 05

The light works well, and I like the shade. How much are you asking?

灯亮得很好，我也喜欢这个灯罩。你打算卖多少钱？

Daniel · 06

I'm asking ten dollars for it.

我想卖十美元。

Tessa · 07

Would you take eight? There's a small scratch on the base.

八美元你愿意卖吗？底座上有一道小划痕。

Daniel · 08

Eight is fine. I hadn't noticed the scratch.

八美元可以。我之前没注意到那道划痕。

Tessa · 09

Thanks. I have cash. Do you have change for a ten?

谢谢。我有现金。你能找开十美元吗？

Daniel · 10

I do. Here's your change, and I'll wrap the cord for you.

能。这是找你的钱，我帮你把电线绕好。

Tessa · 11

That's kind of you. It'll fit perfectly on my table.

你真贴心。它放在我的桌子上正合适。

Daniel · 12

I'm glad it has a new home. Enjoy your reading!

很高兴它有了新主人。祝你阅读愉快！

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① for sale

待售。

例句：

Is that bicycle still for sale?

② turn it on

把它打开，常用于电器。

例句：

Please turn it on to see if the fan works.

③ How much are you asking?

你打算卖多少钱？用于询问卖家的要价。

例句：

How much are you asking for the chair?

④ Would you take... ?

……的价格你愿意接受吗？可用于礼貌议价。

例句：

Would you take five dollars for the basket?

⑤ have change

有零钱可找。

例句：

Do you have change for a twenty?

03 · American Pronunciation

garage 在美式英语中常读作 guh-RAHZH，重音在第二音节；scratch 末尾的 /tʃ/ 像 cheese 开头的音。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Secondhand items

Do you ever buy secondhand things?

Yes. I sometimes buy used books or furniture. I like finding something useful and giving it another home.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A useful secondhand purchase

Describe a secondhand thing you bought. Say where you found it, how you chose it, and why it was useful.

I bought a small reading lamp at a neighborhood garage sale last summer. I needed more light on my desk, so I asked the seller if I could try it. The lamp worked, although there was a scratch on the base. We agreed on eight dollars, and he wrapped the cord for me. I still use it in the evenings when I read or write.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Reusing things

Why do some people prefer buying used items?

Used items may cost less, and buying them can keep useful things in use for longer. Some people also enjoy finding older designs that are hard to find in stores.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读议价部分，再询问一把旧椅子的价格并提出自己的报价。
