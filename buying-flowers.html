SPEAKING LESSON · 42

Flowers for a Thank-You

买一束花表达谢意

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：3 个。

Jules 在花店请店员 Aaron 帮忙挑一束送给老师的花，并选择简单包装。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，练习题为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Jules · 01

I would like some flowers to thank my teacher. Could you help me choose?

我想买些花感谢老师。你能帮我挑选吗？

Aaron · 02

Of course. Do you have a color in mind?

当然。你想好要什么颜色了吗？

Jules · 03

She likes yellow, and I would like something cheerful.

她喜欢黄色，我想要看起来明快的花。

Aaron · 04

These yellow daisies look lovely with the white ones.

这些黄色雏菊和白色的搭配很好看。

Jules · 05

They do. Could you make a small bunch with both colors?

确实。能用这两种颜色扎一小束吗？

Aaron · 06

Certainly. How much would you like to spend?

当然可以。你想花多少钱？

Jules · 07

About twenty dollars, if that is enough for a small bunch.

大约二十美元，如果够买一小束的话。

Aaron · 08

Yes, this bunch comes to eighteen dollars. Would you like a ribbon?

够的，这束十八美元。你想加一条丝带吗？

Jules · 09

A simple green ribbon would be great. Is that included?

一条简单的绿色丝带就很好。这个价格包括丝带吗？

Aaron · 10

Yes, it is. I can wrap the stems in paper, too.

包括的。我还可以用纸把花茎包起来。

Jules · 11

Thank you. I will write her a note when I get home.

谢谢。我回家后会给她写张便条。

Aaron · 12

Here you are. I hope she enjoys the flowers.

给你。希望她喜欢这些花。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① have a color in mind

心里有想好的颜色。

例句：

Do you have a color in mind for the card?

② a small bunch

一小束，常用于花。

例句：

He picked a small bunch of flowers.

③ come to

合计为，用于说明总价。

例句：

The two notebooks come to ten dollars.

④ be included

包含在内。

例句：

Is the card included in the price?

⑤ wrap the stems

包住花茎。

例句：

Please wrap the stems in paper.

03 · American Pronunciation

daisies 的重音在第一音节，结尾发 /z/ 音；bunch 以 /tʃ/ 结尾，与 lunch 的结尾相同。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Flowers

Do you like receiving flowers?

Yes. A small bunch can make a room feel brighter, and I like knowing someone chose it for me.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A thoughtful present

Describe a small present you gave someone. Say who received it, how you chose it, and how they reacted.

I once gave my teacher a small bunch of yellow flowers at the end of a course. I chose yellow because she often wore a yellow scarf. At the flower shop, I asked for a simple ribbon and stayed within my budget. I added a short note to thank her for encouraging me. She smiled and put the flowers on her desk.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Giving

Why do people sometimes value a simple gift?

A simple gift can show that the giver paid attention to the recipient. The thought and the occasion may matter more than the price.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读挑选颜色和询问总价的句子，然后向店员描述你想买的花。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

I would like a small bunch in pink and white. I can spend about twenty dollars. Is the ribbon included?
