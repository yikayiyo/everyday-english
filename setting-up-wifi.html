SPEAKING LESSON · 36

Connecting to the New Wi-Fi

连接新家的无线网络

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：1 个。

新住户 Nina 和室友 Owen 根据路由器标签连接家庭网络并确认连接成功。 本课为 2026-11-22 预制系列原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Nina · 01

I cannot find our new Wi-Fi network on my laptop.

我的笔记本电脑找不到新家的无线网络。

Owen · 02

The router is on. What network names can you see?

路由器开着。你能看到哪些网络名称？

Nina · 03

I see one called Maple Home and another called Maple Guest.

我看到一个叫 Maple Home，另一个叫 Maple Guest。

Owen · 04

Maple Home is ours. The name is on the label under the router.

Maple Home 是我们的。路由器底部的标签上有名称。

Nina · 05

Found it. Is the password on that label, too?

找到了。密码也在标签上吗？

Owen · 06

Yes. Be careful: the last character is a number, not a letter.

是的。注意，最后一个字符是数字，不是字母。

Nina · 07

Thanks. I will type it again slowly.

谢谢。我会再慢慢输入一遍。

Owen · 08

Does the laptop say it is connected now?

笔记本电脑现在显示已连接吗？

Nina · 09

Yes, but let me open a page to check.

显示了，不过让我打开网页确认一下。

Owen · 10

Good idea. My phone connected earlier, so the network is working for me.

好主意。我的手机刚才连上了，所以网络在我这边是正常的。

Nina · 11

The page loaded. I must have typed the password wrong before.

网页加载出来了。我之前肯定是输错密码了。

Owen · 12

Glad it works. Keep the password private when guests visit.

能用了就好。客人来时注意保管好密码。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① network name

网络名称，用来辨认要连接的网络。

例句：

The network name appears on the screen.

② on the label

在标签上。

例句：

The model number is on the label.

③ type it again

再输入一次。

例句：

Please type it again slowly.

④ open a page

打开网页。

例句：

Open a page to test your connection.

⑤ load

加载，指网页内容显示出来。

例句：

The page took a moment to load.

03 · American Pronunciation

router 在美式英语中常读作 ROW-ter；password 重音在第一音节。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Internet

What do you usually use the internet for at home?

I use it to talk with family, read the news, and watch short videos in the evening.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Solving a small problem

Describe a small problem with a device that you solved. Say what happened, what you tried, and how it ended.

When I moved into a new apartment, my laptop would not connect to the Wi-Fi. My roommate showed me the network name and password on the router label. I noticed that I had entered a letter where there was a number. I typed the password again and opened a page to test it. The page loaded, and I felt relieved that we had found the mistake.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Technology

Why can simple instructions make technology easier to use?

Clear instructions help people find the next step without feeling lost. They can also prevent small mistakes that waste time.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读确认网络名称和密码的句子，再描述如何检查连接。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

I can see the network name on the label. I will type the password carefully and open a page to check the connection.
