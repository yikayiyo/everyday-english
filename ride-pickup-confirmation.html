Confirming a Ride Pickup

叫车确认上车地点

A2 → B115–20 分钟补充课程 · 2026-11-22

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听；尚未进行浏览器播放或卡拉OK交互验证。零时长词 0 个，可能无法高亮。

乘客 Maya 和司机 Cole 通过叫车应用联系。Maya 在图书馆东门，Cole 原在西门；他们重新约在东门，并在上车前核对应用中的司机、车辆和目的地。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Maya · 01

Hello, this is Maya. Are you Cole, my driver?

你好，我是 Maya。你是我的司机 Cole 吗？

Cole · 02

Yes, I am Cole. I am at the library west entrance.

是的，我是 Cole。我在图书馆西门。

Maya · 03

I am at the east entrance. Could you meet me here instead?

我在东门。你能改到这里接我吗？

Cole · 04

Yes, I will drive around to the east entrance.

可以，我会开到东门。

Maya · 05

Thank you. I am beside the sign for the east entrance.

谢谢。我在东门标志旁边。

Cole · 06

I see the sign now, and I have stopped beside it.

我现在看到标志了，已经停在旁边。

Maya · 07

Before I get in, my app shows Cole and a blue car. Is that you?

上车前我确认一下：我的应用显示司机是 Cole，车是蓝色的。是你吗？

Cole · 08

Yes. Please also check the license plate against your app.

是的。也请你对照应用核对一下车牌。

Maya · 09

The plate matches. What destination do you have?

车牌一致。你看到的目的地是哪里？

Cole · 10

The City Museum. Is that where you are going?

市博物馆。你要去那里吗？

Maya · 11

Yes, that matches my app. I will get in now.

对，和我应用里的一致。我现在上车。

Cole · 12

Great. The door is unlocked. Welcome aboard.

好的，车门没锁。请上车。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① pickup location

上车地点；用于确认司机在哪里接乘客。

例句：

Is the east entrance your pickup location?

② meet me here

在这里接我或与我会合；可用来重新约地点。

例句：

Could you meet me here instead?

③ drive around

绕路开过去；用于车辆前往建筑物另一侧。

例句：

I will drive around to the east entrance.

④ license plate

车牌；上车前可与应用中的信息核对。

例句：

The license plate matches the app.

⑤ match

一致、相符；用于确认两处信息相同。

例句：

The destination matches my app.

03 · American Pronunciation

entrance /ˈɛntrəns/ 重音在第一音节；vehicle /ˈviːɪkəl/ 重音在第一音节。练习 license plate 时，license 的 c 读 /s/，plate 末尾的 /t/ 不要加元音。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Getting around

How do you usually travel to a new place?

I usually take a bus or request a ride. Before I leave, I check the pickup location and the destination.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A journey

Describe a journey when you had to arrange a pickup. Say where you were, how you communicated, and what happened.

Last weekend I requested a ride from a library to a museum. I waited at the east entrance, but the driver was at the west entrance. I told him where I was and asked him to meet me at the east entrance. Before getting in, I checked his name and the car and plate against the app. We also confirmed the destination, and then I got in.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Travel communication

Why can a clear pickup location matter when people travel?

A clear location saves time and helps people find each other. A recognizable entrance or sign can be more useful than simply naming a large building.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先逐句跟读，再扮演乘客：说明入口不同，重新约定地点，上车前对照应用核对司机、车辆和目的地。
