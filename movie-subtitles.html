Choosing Subtitles for Movie Night

电影之夜选择字幕

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：1 个。

Zoe 和朋友 Ryan 在家看电影，商量字幕设置和暂停方式。本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Zoe · 01

This movie looks interesting. Should we turn on subtitles?

这部电影看起来很有意思。我们打开字幕好吗？

Ryan · 02

Sure. Would you prefer English subtitles or Chinese subtitles?

好。你更喜欢英文字幕还是中文字幕？

Zoe · 03

Let's try English first. I want to follow the actors' words.

先试试英文吧。我想跟上演员说的话。

Ryan · 04

Good idea. Can you read them from the couch?

好主意。你坐在沙发上能看清吗？

Zoe · 05

Almost, but the letters are a little small.

差不多，不过字有点小。

Ryan · 06

I'll make them bigger in the settings. Is that better?

我在设置里把字调大些。这样好点了吗？

Zoe · 07

Much better, thanks. The dialogue is pretty fast, though.

好多了，谢谢。不过对话语速挺快的。

Ryan · 08

We can pause if we miss something.

如果漏听了，我们可以暂停。

Zoe · 09

Could we go back a few seconds? I missed that last line.

能倒回去几秒吗？我没听清刚才那句。

Ryan · 10

Of course. I'll replay it with the subtitles on.

当然。我会开着字幕重放。

Zoe · 11

Now I understand why she left the room.

现在我明白她为什么离开房间了。

Ryan · 12

Great. Let's keep watching, and we can switch languages if we need to.

太好了。我们继续看，如果需要可以切换字幕语言。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① turn on subtitles

打开字幕。

例句：

Turn on subtitles before the show starts.

② follow the words

听懂并跟上所说的话。

例句：

It's easier to follow the words when she speaks slowly.

③ in the settings

在设置中。

例句：

You can change the sound in the settings.

④ go back a few seconds

倒回去几秒。

例句：

Can you go back a few seconds?

⑤ keep watching

继续观看。

例句：

Let's keep watching after the break.

03 · American Pronunciation

subtitles 重读第一音节 SUB；pause 的元音与 caught 的元音在许多美式口音中相同。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Movies

Do you watch movies with subtitles?

Sometimes. I use English subtitles when the actors speak quickly, and I turn them off when I want to focus on listening.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A movie you watched with someone

Describe a movie you watched with someone. Say who was with you, how you watched it, and what you enjoyed.

Last month I watched a mystery movie at home with a friend. We chose English subtitles because some of the actors spoke quickly. Once, we paused and went back to hear a line again. We also talked about what might happen next, but we tried not to interrupt too often. I enjoyed watching with my friend because we noticed different details and could discuss the ending afterward.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Subtitles

How might subtitles change the way people enjoy a movie?

Subtitles can help viewers follow unfamiliar words or hear a story in another language. However, some people may spend more time reading and notice less of the picture.

跟读后与朋友商量字幕语言、字号和漏听时怎么办。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

Let's use English subtitles. Could you make the letters bigger? We can pause if we miss a line.
