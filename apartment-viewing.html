SPEAKING LESSON · 14

Viewing an Apartment

看房租公寓

A2 → B115–20 分钟租房 · 看房

Maya 来到 Oak Street Apartments，由租房经纪人 Sam 带她看三楼的一室公寓。她询问租金是否含水电、是否有室内洗衣机、租约和押金，以及停车方式，并表示如果决定申请，本周就可以提交材料。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Sam · 01

Hi, you must be Maya. I'm Sam, the leasing agent. Welcome to Oak Street Apartments.

你好，你一定是 Maya。我是租房经纪人 Sam。欢迎来到 Oak Street Apartments。

Maya · 02

Hi, Sam. Thanks for showing me around. I'm here to see the one-bedroom on the third floor.

你好，Sam。谢谢你带我看房。我是来看三楼这套一室公寓的。

Sam · 03

Of course. Right this way. The unit is unfurnished, and the rent is eighteen hundred a month.

当然。这边请。这套公寓是空房，不带家具，租金是每月一千八百美元。

Maya · 04

Does that include utilities?

这个价格含水电等杂费吗？

Sam · 05

Water and trash are included, but you'd pay for electricity and internet yourself.

水和垃圾费已经包含在内，不过电费和网费需要你自己付。

Maya · 06

That works for me. Is there in-unit laundry, or do residents share machines?

这对我来说没问题。公寓里有洗衣机和烘干机吗，还是住户共用？

Sam · 07

There's a washer and dryer in the apartment, and the building has a small gym on the first floor.

公寓里有洗衣机和烘干机，大楼一楼还有一个小健身房。

Maya · 08

That's convenient. How long is the lease, and do I need to put down a security deposit?

很方便。租约是多长时间，需要交押金吗？

Sam · 09

It's a twelve-month lease, and the deposit is one month's rent. I'd also need proof of income.

是十二个月的租约，押金为一个月租金。我还需要你提供收入证明。

Maya · 10

I can provide that. Is parking included, or is there a garage?

这个我可以提供。停车包含在内吗，还是有车库？

Sam · 11

A garage space is an extra seventy-five a month, or you can try street parking after six.

车库车位每个月额外七十五美元，或者你可以在六点以后试试路边停车。

Maya · 12

I'll take the garage if I apply. Could I fill out an application this week?

如果我申请的话，我会要车库车位。我这周可以填写申请表吗？

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Unfurnished

不带家具的空房。看房时要先问清楚公寓是否配家具。

例句：

The unit is unfurnished, and the rent is eighteen hundred a month.

② Does that include utilities?

这个价格含水电等杂费吗？utilities 通常指水、电、燃气、垃圾费等。

例句：

Does that include utilities?

③ In-unit laundry

公寓内部的洗衣设备，相对楼内公用洗衣房。

例句：

Is there in-unit laundry, or do residents share machines?

④ Security deposit

租房押金。美国常见为一到两个月租金，退房时按约定退还。

例句：

How long is the lease, and do I need to put down a security deposit?

⑤ Proof of income

收入证明。申请租房时，房东或经纪人常会要求提供。

例句：

It's a twelve-month lease, and the deposit is one month's rent. I'd also need proof of income.

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 utilities 的重音：它落在第二音节，读作 yu-TIL-i-tees /juːˈtɪlətiz/。deposit 的重音也在第二音节，读作 de-POS-it /dɪˈpɑːzɪt/。unfurnished 读作 /ʌnˈfɜːrnɪʃt/，lease 读作 /liːs/。美式英语里 garage 常读作 /ɡəˈrɑːʒ/。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。说水电杂费时突出 yu-TIL-i-tees，说押金时把 deposit 读成 de-POS-it，说车库时把 garage 读成 guh-RAHZH。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Housing

Do you live in a house or an apartment? What do you like about it?

参考回答（入门训练版）

I live in a one-bedroom apartment near my office. I like it because it's quiet and close to the subway, so I don't need a car. The building also has a small gym, which is convenient after work.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Viewing a place to live

Describe a time you looked at an apartment or a house. Say why you went to see it, what you asked about, and whether you decided to take it.

参考回答（入门训练版）

Last month I visited a one-bedroom apartment on Oak Street. The unit was unfurnished, and the rent was eighteen hundred a month, with water and trash included. I asked about the lease, the security deposit, and parking, and I liked that it had in-unit laundry. I decided to apply that week and paid for a garage space.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Choosing a place to live

What do people usually consider when they choose a place to live?

参考回答（入门训练版）

People usually think about the rent, the location, and whether utilities are included. Many also care about parking, laundry, and how long the lease is. Some renters look for a quiet building or a gym, while others just want a place that is close to work or school.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习看房时提问：租金是否含水电、有没有室内洗衣设备、租约和押金，以及停车怎么安排。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意租金、水电杂费、洗衣设备、租约押金和停车安排。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ④、⑥、⑧、⑩、⑫ 句的水电杂费、室内洗衣、租约押金、停车和提交申请表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Maya 和 Sam，把户型、租金、设施和停车换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你会怎么看一套公寓：你要看几室、关心哪些费用和设施，以及你会不会申请。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

Is the apartment furnished, and does the rent include utilities?

Is there in-unit laundry, or do residents share machines?

How long is the lease, and do you need to put down a security deposit?

Is parking included, or would you pay extra for a garage?
