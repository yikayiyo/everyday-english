Finding the Temporary Bus Stop

寻找临时公交站

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Nina 和另一位乘客 Daniel 发现常用站点暂时关闭，一起寻找临时站。本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Nina · 01

The sign says this bus stop is closed today. Do you know where the bus stops now?

牌子上说这个公交站今天关闭。你知道现在车在哪里停吗？

Daniel · 02

I just read the notice. There's a temporary stop across the street.

我刚看了通知。街对面有一个临时站。

Nina · 03

Is it near the coffee shop on the corner?

是在街角咖啡店附近吗？

Daniel · 04

Yes, just beyond it. You can see a yellow sign by the bench.

对，就在咖啡店过去一点。你能看到长椅旁有个黄色标志。

Nina · 05

I'm taking the number six downtown. Does it stop there too?

我要坐六路车去市中心。它也在那里停吗？

Daniel · 06

The notice lists the number six, so it looks like it does.

通知上列了六路车，看来会停。

Nina · 07

Good. Is there a crosswalk nearby?

太好了。附近有人行横道吗？

Daniel · 08

There's one at the traffic light. Let's cross there.

红绿灯那里有一条。我们从那里过吧。

Nina · 09

Thanks. I almost waited at the old stop.

谢谢。我差点就在旧站等了。

Daniel · 10

I did too. The construction surprised me this morning.

我也是。今天早上看到施工让我很意外。

Nina · 11

Here's the yellow sign, and the number six is on it.

黄色标志在这里，上面有六路车。

Daniel · 12

Great. We found it before the bus arrived.

太好了。我们在公交车到达前找到了。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① temporary stop

临时站点。

例句：

The temporary stop is beside the park.

② across the street

在街对面。

例句：

The pharmacy is across the street.

③ just beyond

就在……过去一点。

例句：

The entrance is just beyond the bridge.

④ It looks like it does.

看来是这样。用于根据现有信息判断。

例句：

The notice lists the downtown bus, so it looks like it stops here.

⑤ at the traffic light

在红绿灯处。

例句：

Turn left at the traffic light.

03 · American Pronunciation

temporary 重音在第一个音节；crosswalk 中 cross 和 walk 之间保持清晰的 /s/ 与 /w/。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Buses

Do you often take the bus?

I take the bus a few times a week. It is convenient when I want to go downtown without finding a place to park.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A change in a journey

Describe a time your usual route changed. Say where you were going, what changed, and what you did.

One morning I went to my usual bus stop on my way to work. A notice said the stop was closed because of construction. I read the sign and saw that the temporary stop was across the street. Another passenger and I walked to the traffic light and crossed together. We found the yellow sign and caught the bus there. I was relieved because I still had enough time to get to work.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Transport information

How can transport companies help passengers when stops change?

They can put clear notices at the old stop and show the new location on a map. Updates online can help too, but signs are useful for people who have already arrived.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读后说明旧站关闭，询问临时站的位置、路线是否停靠以及如何过街。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

Where is the temporary stop? Does the number six stop there? Is there a crosswalk nearby?
