SPEAKING LESSON · 46

Making Time for Music Practice

商量音乐练习时间

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：1 个。

公寓室友 Claire 和 Noah 商量键盘练习的时间与音量，照顾彼此的工作安排。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，练习题为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Claire · 01

I started learning the keyboard. Could we talk about a good practice time?

我开始学键盘了。我们能商量一下合适的练习时间吗？

Noah · 02

Sure. I work from home in the afternoons this week.

当然。我这周下午在家工作。

Claire · 03

Would early evening be better for you?

傍晚对你来说会更好吗？

Noah · 04

Yes, after six I am usually finished with calls.

是的，六点以后我的电话会议通常就结束了。

Claire · 05

I could practice for half an hour around six thirty.

我可以六点半左右练习半小时。

Noah · 06

That works. Can you use headphones with the keyboard?

可以。你的键盘能接耳机吗？

Claire · 07

Yes, I can. You may still hear the keys a little.

可以。不过你可能还是会听到一点按键声。

Noah · 08

That should be fine. Thanks for checking with me.

那应该没问题。谢谢你先和我商量。

Claire · 09

If the sound bothers you, please let me know.

如果声音打扰到你，请告诉我。

Noah · 10

I will. On Thursday I have a late call, though.

我会的。不过周四我有一个较晚的电话会议。

Claire · 11

Then I will practice after your call that day. Send me a message when it ends.

那我周四等你电话结束后再练。结束时给我发条消息吧。

Noah · 12

Deal. I hope you enjoy learning your new instrument.

说定了。希望你学新乐器学得开心。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① practice time

练习时间。

例句：

Let us agree on a practice time.

② work from home

在家工作。

例句：

I work from home on Mondays.

③ around six thirty

六点半左右；around 表示大约。

例句：

The lesson begins around six thirty.

④ use headphones

使用耳机。

例句：

I use headphones when I listen at night.

⑤ let me know

告诉我，让我知道。

例句：

Let me know if the time changes.

03 · American Pronunciation

keyboard 重音在第一音节 KEY；headphones 的 head 与 bed 押韵，不读成 heed。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Music

Do you play a musical instrument?

I am learning the keyboard. I practice for short periods because it helps me remember what I learned without getting too tired.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Learning a skill

Describe a skill you are learning. Say how you practice, what is difficult, and why you want to continue.

I recently started learning to play the keyboard at home. I practice for about half an hour in the early evening and use headphones. At first, it was hard to move both hands at the same time. I started with a slow song and repeated a small part each day. I want to continue because I enjoy hearing myself make progress and hope to play for my family.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Shared spaces

How can people balance hobbies and other people's needs at home?

They can discuss when and where the activity will happen. Simple changes, such as using headphones or choosing a different time, can make sharing a home easier.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读商量练习时间的句子，再向室友提出自己的安排。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

Could I practice the keyboard around six? I can use headphones. Please let me know if the sound bothers you.
