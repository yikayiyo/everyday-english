SPEAKING LESSON · 21

Planning a Picnic

计划一次野餐

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Nora 和 Leo 商量周日野餐的食物、地点和雨天安排。课程为 2026-11-22 预制系列，练习题并非官方真题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Nora · 01

Would you like to have a picnic on Sunday?

你周日想去野餐吗？

Leo · 02

I'd love to. Where should we go?

我很乐意。我们去哪里呢？

Nora · 03

There's a grassy area beside the river.

河边有一片草地。

Leo · 04

That sounds nice. Is there any shade?

听起来不错。那里有阴凉的地方吗？

Nora · 05

Yes, there are a few trees near the path.

有，小路附近有几棵树。

Leo · 06

Great. I'll bring sandwiches and some fruit.

太好了。我带三明治和一些水果。

Nora · 07

I'll pack water and a blanket for us to sit on.

我来带水和一条给我们坐的毯子。

Leo · 08

Should we meet at eleven at the bridge?

我们十一点在桥边见好吗？

Nora · 09

Eleven works. What if it rains?

十一点可以。如果下雨怎么办？

Leo · 10

We could eat at my place instead.

我们可以改在我家吃。

Nora · 11

Good idea. Let's check the forecast that morning.

好主意。我们当天早上看看天气预报吧。

Leo · 12

Deal. I'll send you a message before I leave.

说定了。我出门前给你发消息。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① I'd love to.

我很乐意。用于热情地接受邀请。

例句：

I'd love to join you for lunch.

② Is there any shade?

有阴凉处吗？shade 在这里指遮阳处。

例句：

Is there any shade near the playground?

③ I'll pack...

我来打包带上……。用 I'll 主动承担任务。

例句：

I'll pack a bottle of water.

④ What if it rains?

如果下雨怎么办？用 what if 提出可能发生的情况。

例句：

What if the bus is late?

⑤ instead

代替、改为；常放在句末表示换一种选择。

例句：

We can meet indoors instead.

03 · American Pronunciation

在 what if 中让末尾 /t/ 自然接到 if；forecast 重读第一音节 FOR，第二音节较轻。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Outdoor meals

Do you enjoy eating outdoors?

Yes. I like eating outside when the weather is mild. It feels relaxing to sit with friends and enjoy the fresh air.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A pleasant day outside

Describe a pleasant day you spent outdoors. Say where you went, who was with you, and what you did.

Last spring I had lunch by a river with a friend. We brought sandwiches, apples, and water, and we spread a blanket under a tree. The weather was mild, so we could sit there comfortably. After lunch, we walked along the water and watched people passing by. We talked about our plans for the summer. I enjoyed the day because we had time to relax together outside.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Outdoor spaces

How can public outdoor spaces help communities?

They give people a place to meet and exercise without spending much money. Well-maintained spaces can also make a neighborhood more pleasant.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读两人的安排，随后邀请朋友野餐，说明地点、携带物品和雨天备选方案。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

Let's have a picnic by the river. I'll bring fruit and water. If it rains, we can eat at my place.
