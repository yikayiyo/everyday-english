Clarifying a Task at Work

工作中澄清任务与求助

A2 → B115–20 分钟补充课程 · 2026-11-22

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听；尚未进行浏览器播放或卡拉OK交互验证。零时长词 2 个，可能无法高亮。

同事 Ella 和 Noah 正在准备每周销售表。Ella 不理解如何筛选本周数据，请 Noah 示范；她复述步骤、截止时间和发送给 Noah 核对的下一步。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Ella · 01

I am working on the weekly sales table, but I do not understand the filter step.

我正在做每周销售表，但不明白筛选这一步。

Noah · 02

The filter lets you show only this week's entries.

筛选功能可以让你只显示本周的条目。

Ella · 03

Could you show me how to do that?

你能示范一下怎么做吗？

Noah · 04

Sure. Click the date filter, then select this week.

当然。点击日期筛选，再选择本周。

Ella · 05

I see. Do I save the filtered table as a new file?

我明白了。我要把筛选后的表格另存为新文件吗？

Noah · 06

Yes. Save a copy, then send it to me to check.

是的。保存一个副本，然后发给我核对。

Ella · 07

Let me check: filter for this week, save a copy, and send it to you.

我确认一下：筛选本周数据，保存副本，然后发给你。

Noah · 08

That is right. Please send it by three this afternoon.

对。请在今天下午三点前发给我。

Ella · 09

You will have it by three today. I will send it to you first.

你今天三点前就能收到。我会先把它发给你。

Noah · 10

Exactly. I will check it before we share it with the team.

没错。我会在我们分享给团队之前核对。

Ella · 11

I have applied the filter and saved the copy. I will send it now.

我已经筛选并保存了副本，现在发给你。

Noah · 12

Thanks, Ella. I will look for it and let you know after I check it.

谢谢，Ella。我会留意接收，核对后告诉你。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① filter for

筛选出符合条件的数据；常用于表格。

例句：

Filter for this week's entries.

② Could you show me how?

你能示范一下怎么做吗？不理解步骤时可以使用。

例句：

Could you show me how to use the filter?

③ save a copy

保存一个副本；保留原文件并另存一份。

例句：

Please save a copy before you send it.

④ Let me check

我确认一下；复述前用来核对理解。

例句：

Let me check: you need it by three?

⑤ by three

三点前，最迟三点；by 表示截止时间。

例句：

Please send me the table by three.

03 · American Pronunciation

filter /ˈfɪltər/ 重音在第一音节，末尾 /r/ 可轻卷舌；copy /ˈkɑːpi/ 重音在第一音节。说 by three 时，注意 three 的 /θ/：舌尖轻触上下齿之间。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Learning at work

What do you do when you do not understand a task?

I ask a coworker to show me the step I do not understand. Then I explain it back to make sure I can do it.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Learning a task

Describe a task someone helped you learn. Say what the task was, what help you received, and what you did next.

At work I once needed to prepare a weekly sales table. I did not know how to filter the dates, so I asked a coworker to show me. He clicked the date filter and selected the current week. I repeated the steps and checked when he needed the file. Then I saved a copy and sent it to him before the deadline. His explanation helped me feel more confident.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Asking for help

How can coworkers make it easier to ask for help?

They can listen without making someone feel embarrassed and demonstrate one step at a time. Checking the deadline and the next action also helps everyone understand the task.
