SPEAKING LESSON · 41

Finding the Music at a Street Festival

在街头节庆寻找音乐表演

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：1 个。

Lena 和 Marco 到街头节庆看演出，先确认舞台位置，再商量碰头地点。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，练习题为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Lena · 01

The street festival is busier than I expected. Can you see the music stage?

街头节庆比我想的热闹。你能看到音乐舞台吗？

Marco · 02

Not from here. The map shows it beyond the art stalls.

从这里看不到。地图显示它在艺术摊位那边。

Lena · 03

The next band starts soon, so let us head that way.

下一支乐队很快就要开始了，我们往那边走吧。

Marco · 04

Good idea. Do you want to stop at the craft booth first?

好主意。你想先去手工摊看看吗？

Lena · 05

Maybe afterward. I don't want to miss the first song.

也许演出后吧。我不想错过第一首歌。

Marco · 06

That makes sense. People are moving slowly around the corner.

有道理。拐角附近的人走得很慢。

Lena · 07

If we get separated, let us meet by the blue information tent.

如果我们走散了，就在蓝色服务帐篷旁碰头吧。

Marco · 08

I can see it from here. I will take a picture so I remember.

我从这里能看到。我拍张照，免得忘了。

Lena · 09

Look, the stage is just past that painted sign.

看，舞台就在那块彩绘指示牌过去一点。

Marco · 10

And there is room to stand near the back. Can you see from there?

后面附近有地方站。你从那里能看见吗？

Lena · 11

Yes, and we can hear the music without being too close.

能，而且不用靠得太近就能听到音乐。

Marco · 12

Perfect. Let's enjoy the band and visit the booths later.

太好了。我们先欣赏乐队，待会儿再逛摊位。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① head that way

朝那个方向走。

例句：

The entrance is ahead, so let us head that way.

② miss the first song

错过第一首歌；miss 可表示错过活动。

例句：

We left early so we would not miss the first song.

③ get separated

走散。

例句：

We got separated in the crowd.

④ just past

刚过……一点。

例句：

The cafe is just past the bookstore.

⑤ near the back

靠近后方。

例句：

We found two seats near the back.

03 · American Pronunciation

festival 的重音在第一个音节；stage 结尾是 /dʒ/，与 age 的结尾相同。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Festivals

Do you enjoy outdoor festivals?

Yes, especially when there is live music. I like walking around and seeing what local artists have made.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A public event

Describe a public event you enjoyed. Say where it was, what you did there, and why you remember it.

I went to a street festival with a friend last summer. We followed a map to a small stage and listened to a local band. After the music, we looked at handmade bowls and posters at the nearby booths. The street was crowded, but everyone seemed cheerful. I remember singing along to a song I already knew and laughing with my friend.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Local events

How can local festivals bring people together?

Festivals give residents a reason to share a public space and discover local artists. Clear signs and accessible routes can also help more people take part.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读问路和约定碰头地点的句子，再描述你想看的演出。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

Where is the music stage? Let us meet by the information tent if we get separated. We can visit the craft booths afterward.
