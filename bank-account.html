SPEAKING LESSON · 11

Opening a Bank Account

在银行开户

A2 → B115–20 分钟银行 · 开户

Ava 刚搬到附近，走进银行想开一个支票账户；她向柜员 Mason 说明是第一次办理，出示驾照和账单作为身份与地址证明，并询问借记卡、最低存款和日后如何查询余额。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Ava · 01

Hi. I'd like to open a checking account, please.

你好。我想开一个支票账户。

Mason · 02

Of course. Have you banked with us before?

当然可以。你以前在我们这里办过业务吗？

Ava · 03

No, this is my first time. I just moved to the neighborhood.

没有，这是第一次。我刚搬到这附近。

Mason · 04

Welcome. I'll need a photo ID and proof of your current address.

欢迎。我需要一份带照片的身份证明，以及你现在住址的证明。

Ava · 05

Sure. I have my driver's license and a utility bill.

好的。我带了驾照和一份水电账单。

Mason · 06

That's perfect. There's no monthly fee if you set up direct deposit.

非常合适。如果你设置工资直存，就没有月费。

Ava · 07

That works for me. Can I get a debit card today?

这对我没问题。今天能拿到借记卡吗？

Mason · 08

The card will be mailed in five to seven days. You can use it as soon as it arrives.

卡会在五到七天内寄出。寄到后就可以用了。

Ava · 09

Okay. Is there a minimum deposit to open the account?

好的。开户有最低存款要求吗？

Mason · 10

Yes. You can start with twenty-five dollars today.

有的。今天先存二十五美元就可以了。

Ava · 11

Great. I'll deposit that now. How do I check my balance later?

太好了。我现在就存。以后怎么查询余额呢？

Mason · 12

You can use our mobile app, or check at an ATM anytime.

你可以用我们的手机应用，也可以随时在自动取款机上查询。

听力理解

不看中文，再听一遍对话，尝试回答：

What does Ava need to open the account, and how can she check her balance later?

Ava 开户需要带什么？以后怎么查询余额？

查看答案

她需要带照片的身份证明和现在住址的证明；她带了驾照和一份水电账单。设置工资直存就没有月费。借记卡会在五到七天内寄到，寄到后就可以用。开户最低存款是二十五美元。以后可以用银行的手机应用，或随时在自动取款机上查询余额。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Open a checking account

开一个支票账户。在银行说明办理业务时常用。

例句：

I'd like to open a checking account, please.

② Have you banked with us before?

你以前在我们这里办过业务吗？工作人员确认是否为新客户时常用。

例句：

Have you banked with us before?

③ Proof of your current address

现在住址的证明。开户时常用水电账单或租约来证明住址。

例句：

I'll need a photo ID and proof of your current address.

④ Set up direct deposit

设置工资或收入直存。银行说明免月费条件时常用。

例句：

There's no monthly fee if you set up direct deposit.

⑤ Minimum deposit

最低存款。询问或说明开户起步金额时常用。

例句：

Is there a minimum deposit to open the account?

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 checking account 的节奏：CHECKing acCOUNT。注意 deposit 作名词时重音在前：DE-po-sit，debit 的重音在前：DE-bit，以及 utility 的重音在第二音节：u-TI-li-ty。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。说账户时突出 CHECKing account，说存款时突出 DE-po-sit，说借记卡时突出 DE-bit card。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Banks and money

Do you usually pay with a bank card, or do you still use cash? Why?

参考回答（入门训练版）

I usually pay with a debit card because it is convenient and I can check my balance in an app. I only use cash for small things, like a snack or a bus ticket.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A time you visited a bank

Describe a time you went to a bank. Say why you went, what you needed to bring, and what happened.

参考回答（入门训练版）

Last month I went to a bank to open a checking account after I moved. I brought my driver's license and a utility bill. The clerk said there was no monthly fee with direct deposit, and my debit card would arrive in about a week.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Banking in daily life

Why do some people still visit a bank in person instead of using an app?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Some people want to ask questions face to face, especially when they open an account or have a problem. Others may feel apps are hard to use, or they prefer to handle money in person. Banks in the neighborhood can also feel more trustworthy for important tasks.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习说明你要开什么账户、需要带哪些证件，以及如何查询余额。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意账户类型、证件要求、月费条件和查询余额的方式。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ①、④、⑥、⑨、⑫ 句的开户、证明、直存、最低存款和查余额表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Ava 和 Mason，把账户类型、证件和起步金额换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你会怎么在银行开户：账户类型、身份和地址证明、最低存款，以及日后如何查余额。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

What kind of account would you open?

What ID and proof of address would you bring?

Is there a minimum deposit, and how much would you put in today?

How would you check your balance later?
