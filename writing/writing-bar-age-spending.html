Monthly spending by age group in City X (2025)

X 市两组年龄月均开销（2025）

题目 · Prompt

The bar chart below shows the average monthly spending (in US dollars) of two age groups in City X on four categories in 2025: housing, food, transport, and leisure.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

范文 · Sample essay

The bar chart compares the average monthly expenditure of people aged 25–34 and 55–64 in City X across four categories in 2025.

Overall, the younger group spent more than the older group in every category. Housing was by far the largest cost for both age groups, while transport accounted for the smallest share of spending among those aged 55–64.

Looking at housing first, 25-to-34-year-olds spent $1,200 a month on average, compared with $900 for the 55–64 group. Food followed a similar pattern, though the gap was narrower: $450 versus $380. For transport, younger adults spent nearly twice as much as older adults ($280 against $150). Leisure spending also favoured the younger cohort, at $320 compared with $210.

In summary, while both groups allocated the most money to housing and the least (among older adults) to transport, people in their mid-twenties to mid-thirties consistently outspent those approaching retirement age across all four categories.

中文点拨 / 实用表达

  1. Overall, A ... while B ... — 总述对比。
  2. by far the largest — 强调遥遥领先。
  3. followed a similar pattern, though the gap was narrower — 结构相似但差距更小。
  4. consistently outspent — 各项都花得更多，适合收束。
