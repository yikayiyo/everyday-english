# IELTS Academic Task 1 — Pie chart (2026-10-26)

**说明：** 以下题目与数据均为练习用**示例数据**，不是官方真题。

### 题目（文字描述）

The pie chart below shows the share of electricity generated from different sources in Country X in 2025.

**示例数据（占比）：**
- Natural gas: 35%
- Coal: 20%
- Nuclear: 15%
- Hydro: 12%
- Solar: 10%
- Wind: 8%

### 范文（约 160 词）

The pie chart illustrates the proportion of electricity produced from six energy sources in Country X in 2025.

Overall, fossil fuels still accounted for over half of the country’s electricity, with natural gas alone making up the largest single share, while renewable sources other than hydro remained relatively modest.

Natural gas was the dominant source at 35%, followed by coal at 20%. Together, these two fossil fuels generated 55% of the total. Nuclear power contributed a further 15%, which was slightly higher than hydroelectricity at 12%.

Among the remaining renewables, solar and wind played smaller roles, at 10% and 8% respectively. Combined, solar and wind still produced less electricity than coal alone. Hydro was the largest renewable contributor in the chart, yet it was still well below natural gas.

In summary, Country X’s electricity mix in 2025 was led by natural gas, with coal as the second main source, while nuclear and renewables made up the rest in descending order of share.

### 可借鉴的表达（中文点拨）

1. **The pie chart illustrates…** — 开篇客观引出图表内容，比单纯写 *shows* 稍正式。
2. **accounted for / making up the largest single share** — 写占比常用：*account for* 表示“占……比例”，*the largest single share* 突出“单项最大”。
3. **Together, these two… generated 55% of the total.** — 把相关类别合并比较，体现“分组对比”。
4. **in descending order of share** — 结尾按占比从高到低收束，结构清晰。
