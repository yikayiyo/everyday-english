# IELTS Academic Task 1 · Line graph · 2026-10-20

**Chart type:** line
**Title EN:** Teenagers’ daily leisure time (Country X, 2000–2020)
**Title ZH:** 青少年每日休闲时间（X 国，2000–2020）
**Slug:** writing-line-teen-leisure
**Note:** 示例数据，非官方真题。

## Prompt
The line graph below shows the average amount of time teenagers in Country X spent per day on three leisure activities between 2000 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

## Sample data (minutes per day)
- Watching TV: 2000=150, 2005=140, 2010=120, 2015=90, 2020=70
- Social media: 2000=5, 2005=20, 2010=60, 2015=110, 2020=150
- Reading for pleasure: 2000=40, 2005=38, 2010=35, 2015=30, 2020=25

## Sample essay
The line graph illustrates how much time teenagers in Country X spent each day on three leisure activities, namely watching television, using social media and reading for pleasure, between 2000 and 2020.

Overall, social media use rose dramatically and became the most time-consuming activity, while both television viewing and reading declined over the period.

In 2000, watching television was by far the dominant pastime, accounting for 150 minutes a day, compared with just 40 minutes for reading and a mere 5 minutes for social media. Over the following two decades, TV viewing fell steadily, dropping to 120 minutes in 2010 and then more sharply to 70 minutes by 2020.

Social media, by contrast, showed the opposite trend. After a modest rise to 20 minutes in 2005, the figure tripled to 60 minutes in 2010 and continued to climb rapidly. It overtook television at some point between 2010 and 2015, and by 2020 it had reached 150 minutes, exactly the level TV had recorded at the start of the period.

Reading for pleasure saw only a gradual decline, slipping from 40 to 25 minutes, and it remained the least popular of the three activities from 2010 onwards.

## Chinese tips
1. **Overall, A rose dramatically …, while B and C declined …** — 一句话总述最大趋势。
2. **by far the dominant … , accounting for …** — 强调遥遥领先并给出占比/数值。
3. **by contrast, showed the opposite trend / overtook** — 转写相反趋势或交叉。
4. **the figure tripled / slipping from 40 to 25** — 倍数与不同程度的升降动词。
