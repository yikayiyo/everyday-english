# IELTS Academic Task 1 — Map (2026-11-01)

**题型说明（中文）**：今天练 **地图对比**（两个时间点的平面布局变化）。请先看清方位（南北入口、东北角等），再写总体变化 + 分点细节；不要写个人观点。

**图意文字描述（示例数据，非真题）**  
某中国城市一处住宅小区平面图，对比 **2016** 与 **2026**：  
- **2016**：南侧唯一大门；中心为大片地面停车场；东北角小型儿童乐园；西侧开放绿地；无快递柜、无共享单车停靠点。  
- **2026**：中心停车场改为步道与座椅；西侧绿地下方新增地下车库；南门旁新增一排智能快递柜与共享单车停靠点；东南角新增社区咖啡馆；东北儿童乐园扩大，旁侧新增社区菜园。

**English question prompt**  
The maps below show the layout of a residential compound in a Chinese city in 2016 and in 2026.  
*(Sample data for practice only — not an official past paper.)*  
Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.  
Write at least 150 words.

**Sample essay (full English)**  
The two maps illustrate how a residential compound in a Chinese city changed between 2016 and 2026 (sample data for practice only).

Overall, the compound became more service-oriented and pedestrian-friendly. Parking moved underground, while new facilities such as parcel lockers, a shared-bike dock and a small café were added near the main entrance.

In 2016, a large surface car park occupied the centre of the compound, and the only entrance was on the south side. A small playground stood in the north-east corner, and an open green area lay to the west. There were no lockers or bike docks, so residents had limited options for collecting deliveries or short local trips.

By 2026, the central car park had been replaced by landscaped paths and seating. Vehicles were redirected to a new underground car park under the west green space. Beside the southern entrance, a row of smart parcel lockers and a shared-bike dock appeared, and a café opened on the south-east corner. The playground in the north-east was enlarged, and a community garden was created next to it. These changes made daily errands such as picking up packages more convenient while freeing the centre for walking and social use.

### 中文点评（可复用句型/词汇）
1. **总述句型**：*Overall, … became more … while … were added/relocated.* — 先抓“变友好/功能更多 + 一处关键迁移”。  
2. **方位描写**：*in the north-east corner / beside the southern entrance / under the west green space* — 地图题靠方位词把变化钉死。  
3. **变化动词**：*was replaced by / were redirected to / appeared / was enlarged / was created* — 被动 + 清晰动作，比反复 *there is* 更像高分写法。  
4. **对比收束**：*These changes made … more convenient while freeing … for …* — 用结果句收束，避免流水账罗列。
