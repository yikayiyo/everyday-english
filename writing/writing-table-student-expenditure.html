Average monthly expenditure of university students in four cities (2025)

四城市大学生五类月均开销（2025）

题目 · Prompt

The table below shows the average monthly expenditure (in US dollars) of university students in four cities on five categories in 2025. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

示例数据 · Sample data

CategoryTokyoLondonSydneyBerlin
Food280320300250
Rent650900750500
Transport8012010070
Study materials40554535
Leisure100150130110

范文 · Sample essay

The table compares how much university students in Tokyo, London, Sydney and Berlin spent on average each month on five categories in 2025.

Overall, rent was by far the largest expense in every city, while study materials accounted for the smallest share. Students in London spent the most across almost all categories, whereas those in Berlin generally spent the least.

Rent ranged from $500 in Berlin to $900 in London, with Tokyo and Sydney in between at $650 and $750 respectively. Food was the second-highest cost everywhere, peaking at $320 in London and falling to $250 in Berlin. Leisure spending followed a similar pattern: London led at $150, followed by Sydney ($130), Berlin ($110) and Tokyo ($100).

Transport costs were relatively modest, from $70 in Berlin to $120 in London. Study materials remained under $60 in all four cities, with Berlin again lowest at $35 and London highest at $55.

In summary, housing dominated monthly budgets, and London was consistently the most expensive of the four cities for students.

中文点拨 / 实用表达

  • Overall, X was by far the largest…, while Y accounted for the smallest share.：先总述极值，再点出占比最小的一项。
  • ranged from… to…, with A and B in between at… respectively：写连续区间并点名中间两项。
  • followed a similar pattern：承接上一类数据，说明走势相近，避免句式重复。
  • consistently the most expensive：收束段点明贯穿全文的对比结论。
