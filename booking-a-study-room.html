Booking a Study Room

在图书馆预约研讨室

A2 → B115–20 分钟图书馆 · 预约

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听；尚未进行浏览器播放或卡拉OK交互验证。第 1、8、12 句共有 3 个零时长词（reserve、drawer、hour），可能无法高亮。第 10 句 Whisper 原稿漏了句首 We hold the room for ten minutes，对齐后这些词仍在，但前半句不少词时长被压到约 0.001 秒，高亮可能不准。

Maya 下午要和同学做小组作业，到图书馆前台预约研讨室；工作人员 Jordan 帮她确认人数、使用时长和房间设施，并说明迟到保留与取消规则。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Maya · 01

Hi, I'd like to reserve a study room for this afternoon, please.

你好，我想预约一间今天下午的研讨室。

Jordan · 02

Sure. How many people will be using it, and for how long?

好的。你们几个人用，需要用多久？

Maya · 03

There will be four of us, and we need it for two hours.

我们有四个人，需要用两个小时。

Jordan · 04

I have a room on the third floor from 2:00 to 4:00. Does that work for you?

三楼有一间，下午两点到四点。这个时间可以吗？

Maya · 05

That works. Do I need a library card to book it?

可以。预约需要借书证吗？

Jordan · 06

Yes, I'll need to see your library card. The room holds up to six people, so you'll have some extra space.

需要，请出示一下你的借书证。这间最多可以坐六个人，所以你们还会有一点空余位置。

Maya · 07

Perfect. Can we use the whiteboard and the screen in the room?

太好了。房间里的白板和屏幕我们可以用吗？

Jordan · 08

Yes. There are markers in the drawer, and you can plug your laptop into the screen.

可以。白板笔在抽屉里，你也可以把笔记本电脑接到屏幕上。

Maya · 09

Great. What if we're a few minutes late?

好的。如果我们晚到几分钟怎么办？

Jordan · 10

We hold the room for ten minutes. After that, we may give it to someone else.

房间会为你们保留十分钟。超过这个时间，我们可能会把房间给其他人。

Maya · 11

Got it. Can I cancel if our plans change?

明白了。如果我们计划有变，可以取消吗？

Jordan · 12

Yes, please cancel online or at the desk at least an hour ahead. You're all set. Here's your confirmation.

可以，请至少提前一小时在网上或到前台取消。都办好了。这是你的确认单。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① I'd like to reserve a study room

我想预约一间研讨室，在图书馆前台说明需求时常用。

例句：

Hi, I'd like to reserve a study room for this afternoon, please.

② Does that work for you?

这个安排可以吗？用来确认时间或方案是否合适。

例句：

I have a room on the third floor from 2:00 to 4:00. Does that work for you?

③ We hold the room for ten minutes

房间会先为你保留十分钟，说明迟到宽限时常用。

例句：

We hold the room for ten minutes. After that, we may give it to someone else.

④ at least an hour ahead

至少提前一小时，取消或改期时常用。

例句：

Please cancel online or at the desk at least an hour ahead.

⑤ You're all set

都办好了，表示事情已经安排妥当。

例句：

You're all set. Here's your confirmation.

03 · American Pronunciation

reserve 的重音在第二音节，读作 /rɪˈzɜːrv/；third 的 th 读作 /θ/，floor 读作 /flɔːr/；whiteboard 读作 /ˈwaɪtbɔːrd/；confirmation 的重音在第三音节，读作 /ˌkɑːnfərˈmeɪʃn/；cancel 的重音在第一音节，读作 /ˈkænsl/。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Libraries

Do you often study in a library?

Yes, I do. I usually go to the library a few times a week when I have group work or need a quiet place. I sometimes book a study room so my classmates and I can talk without bothering other people.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Booking a study room

Describe an imagined time you booked a study room at a library. Say why you needed it, how you reserved it, and what the staff told you about the room.

Imagine that I am at the library desk to reserve a study room for four classmates. I ask for a two-hour booking in the afternoon, and the staff offers a room on the third floor from 2:00 to 4:00. I show my library card, and I also ask if we can use the whiteboard and the screen. The staff tells me they will hold the room for ten minutes if we are late, and I should cancel at least an hour ahead if our plans change.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Study spaces

Why do some people prefer studying in a library instead of at home?

A library is usually quieter than home, and it can be easier to focus when other people are studying too. Some libraries also have study rooms, screens, and a stable internet connection, which are useful for group work. At home it is more comfortable, but it is easier to get distracted.
