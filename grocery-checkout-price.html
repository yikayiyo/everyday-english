Checking Out and Clarifying a Price

超市结账与价格核对

A2 → B115–20 分钟补充课程 · 2026-11-22

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听；尚未进行浏览器播放或卡拉OK交互验证。零时长词 0 个，可能无法高亮。

顾客 Amy 在超市收银台发现一盒麦片扫描价高于货架标价。收银员 Leo 核实标价后修改价格，Amy 核对新价格并刷卡付款。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Amy · 01

The cereal scanned at six dollars, but I saw five dollars on the shelf.

这盒麦片扫出来是六美元，但我在货架上看到的是五美元。

Leo · 02

Let me check the shelf label for that cereal.

我来核对一下那款麦片的货架标签。

Amy · 03

Thank you. It was the label under the large box.

谢谢。标签在大盒麦片的下面。

Leo · 04

I checked it. The label does say five dollars for this box.

我查过了。标签上这盒确实是五美元。

Amy · 05

Could you change the price, please?

请问你能修改价格吗？

Leo · 06

Yes. I have changed it from six dollars to five.

可以。我已经从六美元改成五美元了。

Amy · 07

I can see five dollars on the screen now. Thank you.

我现在看到屏幕上是五美元了。谢谢。

Leo · 08

You are welcome. That is the last item.

不客气。这是最后一件商品。

Amy · 09

Can I pay with a card?

我可以刷卡吗？

Leo · 10

Yes. Please insert your card in the reader.

可以。请把卡插入读卡器。

Amy · 11

The payment went through. May I have a receipt?

付款成功了。我可以要一张收据吗？

Leo · 12

Of course. Here is your receipt. Have a good day.

当然可以。这是你的收据。祝你今天愉快。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① scan at

扫描显示为某个价格；用于描述收银机上的价格。

例句：

This item scanned at six dollars.

② shelf label

货架标签；可用来指明看到的标价。

例句：

Could you check the shelf label?

③ change the price

修改价格；询问收银员能否更正时使用。

例句：

Could you change the price, please?

④ pay with a card

刷卡付款；询问付款方式。

例句：

Can I pay with a card?

⑤ go through

成功完成；付款时表示交易已成功。

例句：

The payment went through.

03 · American Pronunciation

price /praɪs/ 和 receipt /rɪˈsiːt/ 都有清晰的 /s/；receipt 中的 p 不发音，重音在第二音节。练习 shelf label 时分别读清两个词的开头。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Shopping

Do you check prices when you shop for food?

Yes. I compare prices on the shelf and look at the screen at checkout. It helps me notice a mistake.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A shopping experience

Describe a time you checked a price while shopping. Say what you bought, what you noticed, and what happened.

I bought a box of cereal at a supermarket near my home. At checkout, I noticed that the screen showed a higher price than the shelf label. I told the cashier what I had seen and asked her to check. She looked at the label and changed the price. I checked the screen again, paid by card, and thanked her for her help.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Prices in shops

Why is it useful for stores to show prices clearly?

Clear prices help customers decide what to buy and plan their spending. They also make it easier for staff and customers to notice and correct mistakes.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先逐句跟读，再扮演顾客说明货架价与扫描价不同，请对方核对并确认付款方式。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 4–6 句练习；同伴扮演收银员，核对并告知修改后的价格。
