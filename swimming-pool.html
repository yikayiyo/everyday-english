SPEAKING LESSON · 26

A First Visit to the Pool

第一次去游泳池

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Grace 和工作人员 Tom 在游泳池入口讨论泳道与储物安排。本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Grace · 01

I'm visiting this pool for the first time. Where can I swim slowly?

我是第一次来这个游泳池。哪里适合慢游？

Tom · 02

The lane closest to the wall is for slower swimmers this afternoon.

今天下午靠墙的泳道供游得较慢的人使用。

Grace · 03

Thanks. Is there a place to put my bag?

谢谢。有地方放我的包吗？

Tom · 04

Yes, there are lockers in the changing room.

有，更衣室里有储物柜。

Grace · 05

Do I need to bring a lock?

我需要自带锁吗？

Tom · 06

For these lockers, yes. We also sell simple locks at the desk.

这些柜子需要。我们柜台也卖简单的锁。

Grace · 07

I brought one, so that works. Where are the showers?

我带了一把，那就没问题。淋浴间在哪里？

Tom · 08

They're just past the lockers, on your left.

刚过储物柜，在左手边。

Grace · 09

And when does the pool close today?

那今天游泳池什么时候关闭？

Tom · 10

The pool closes at eight tonight, and the changing room closes at eight thirty.

今晚泳池八点关闭，更衣室八点半关闭。

Grace · 11

Great. I'll have plenty of time for a short swim.

太好了。我有充足的时间游一会儿。

Tom · 12

Enjoy your swim. Let me know if you need anything else.

祝你游得愉快。需要别的帮助请告诉我。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① for the first time

第一次。

例句：

I'm visiting this town for the first time.

② closest to

离……最近。

例句：

Choose the chair closest to the door.

③ changing room

更衣室。

例句：

The changing room is down the hall.

④ just past

刚过……的位置。

例句：

The cafe is just past the bank.

⑤ plenty of time

充足的时间。

例句：

We have plenty of time before lunch.

03 · American Pronunciation

swim 的开头依次发 /s/、/w/；lockers 重读第一音节，词尾 r 用美式卷舌音。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Swimming

Do you enjoy swimming?

Yes, I enjoy a slow swim when I have time. It helps me relax, and I like being in the water.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A place to exercise

Describe a place where you exercise. Say where it is, what you do there, and why you like it.

I sometimes go to a swimming pool near my home. I usually visit in the early evening and swim slowly for a while. Before I get into the water, I put my bag in a locker and use the shower. The pool has several lanes, so I choose one that suits my speed. I like the place because it is bright and I can relax after a busy day.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Exercise facilities

How can exercise facilities welcome people with different abilities?

They can offer clear information about activities and provide spaces for different speeds. Friendly staff can also help visitors find an activity that feels comfortable for them.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读后询问适合自己的泳道、储物柜和当天的关闭时间。

08 · 今日口头回答练习
