SPEAKING LESSON · 51

Choosing Supplies for a Drawing

挑选绘画用品

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Lena 在画材店向店员 Max 寻找适合铅笔画的纸和橡皮。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目是原创口语练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Lena · 01

I'm looking for paper for a pencil drawing. Can you help?

我在找适合铅笔画的纸。你能帮忙吗？

Max · 02

Of course. Do you want loose sheets or a sketchbook?

当然。你想要单张纸还是速写本？

Lena · 03

A sketchbook would be easier to carry to the park.

速写本带去公园会更方便。

Max · 04

This one has thicker paper and a hard cover.

这本纸较厚，还有硬封面。

Lena · 05

Can I erase pencil marks without damaging the paper?

擦掉铅笔痕迹时会弄坏纸吗？

Max · 06

If you erase gently, this paper should hold up well.

如果轻轻擦，这种纸应该比较耐用。

Lena · 07

That sounds useful. Do you have a soft eraser too?

听起来很实用。你们也有软橡皮吗？

Max · 08

Yes, these white ones are popular for pencil work.

有，这些白色橡皮画铅笔画的人很喜欢用。

Lena · 09

I'll take one. Are the pencils sold separately?

我拿一块。铅笔是单独卖的吗？

Max · 10

They are. You can try a few different ones at that table.

是的。你可以在那张桌子旁试试几种不同的铅笔。

Lena · 11

I like this darker one, so I'll add it to my basket.

我喜欢这支颜色深一些的，我把它也放进篮子。

Max · 12

Nice choice. I can ring those up at the counter.

选得不错。我可以在柜台帮你结账。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① loose sheets

单张纸，区别于装订成册的纸。

例句：

I keep loose sheets in a folder.

② hold up

保持完好、经得住使用。

例句：

These shoes have held up well.

③ pencil marks

铅笔留下的痕迹。

例句：

There are pencil marks on the page.

④ sold separately

单独出售。

例句：

The brushes are sold separately.

⑤ ring up

在收银台结账。

例句：

The clerk rang up my notebook.

03 · American Pronunciation

sketchbook 的重音在 sketch；separately 的重音在第一个音节，注意中间音节较轻。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Drawing

Do you ever draw in your free time?

Sometimes I draw simple plants in a notebook. It helps me slow down after work.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Something you made

Describe something you made by hand. Say what it was, what you used, and how you felt about it.

I made a pencil drawing of a tree outside my window. I used a small sketchbook, a dark pencil, and a soft eraser. At first the branches looked uneven, so I erased a few lines and tried again. I spent a quiet afternoon on it and gave the finished picture to my sister. I felt pleased because I had made something personal.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Creative activities

Why might adults enjoy making art even if they are beginners?

Art can be relaxing and offers a way to express ideas. Beginners can also enjoy seeing their skills improve without needing to produce perfect work.

跟读后向店员说明你想画什么，询问纸和铅笔。
