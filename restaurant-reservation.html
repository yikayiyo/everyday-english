SPEAKING LESSON · 13

Making a Reservation at a Restaurant

在餐厅预订座位

A2 → B115–20 分钟餐厅 · 订位

Maya 打电话给 Harvest Table 餐厅，向工作人员 Drew 预订周六四人桌。七点半已满，她改订八点的室内安静座位，说明是朋友生日，询问是否可以自带蛋糕，并确认留位时间和停车信息。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Drew · 01

Good evening, Harvest Table. This is Drew. How can I help you?

晚上好，这里是 Harvest Table。我是 Drew。有什么可以帮您？

Maya · 02

Hi. I'd like to make a reservation, please.

你好。我想预订一个位子。

Drew · 03

Of course. For how many people, and which day?

当然可以。几位，哪一天？

Maya · 04

A table for four, this Saturday.

周六，四个人。

Drew · 05

Saturday is pretty busy. What time were you hoping for?

周六会比较忙。你希望大概几点呢？

Maya · 06

Around seven-thirty, if that's possible.

如果可以的话，大概七点半。

Drew · 07

Seven-thirty is fully booked, but I still have a table at eight. Would you like indoor or patio seating?

七点半已经订满了，不过八点还有一张桌子。你想坐室内，还是露台？

Maya · 08

Eight o'clock indoors, please. It's my friend's birthday, so a quieter table would be great.

请给我八点的室内座位。这是我朋友的生日，所以安静一点的位子会更好。

Drew · 09

I can note that and put you in a corner booth. May I have your name and a callback number?

我可以记下来，并给你们安排一个靠角落的卡座。可以告诉我你的名字和回拨电话吗？

Maya · 10

It's Maya Chen, and you can reach me at this number. Can we bring a small cake?

我叫 Maya Chen，用这个号码联系我就行。我们可以自带一个小蛋糕吗？

Drew · 11

Yes, you're welcome to bring a cake. We hold the table for fifteen minutes, and there's street parking out front.

可以，欢迎你们自带蛋糕。座位会为你们保留十五分钟，门口有路边停车位。

Maya · 12

Perfect. Thank you so much. We'll see you Saturday at eight.

太好了。非常感谢。那我们周六八点见。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Make a reservation

预订座位。打电话或在餐厅说明来意时常用。

例句：

I'd like to make a reservation, please.

② A table for four

四个人的桌子。预订时要说明人数和日期。

例句：

A table for four, this Saturday.

③ Fully booked

已经订满。某个时间没有空位时常用，后面常接可改订的时间。

例句：

Seven-thirty is fully booked, but I still have a table at eight.

④ Indoor or patio seating

室内座位还是露台座位。选择用餐位置时常用。patio 指室外或半室外的露台。

例句：

Would you like indoor or patio seating?

⑤ Hold the table

为客人保留座位。餐厅通常会说明最长等待时间。

例句：

We hold the table for fifteen minutes, and there's street parking out front.

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 reservation 的重音：它落在第三音节，读作 re-ser-VA-tion。注意 patio 读作 /ˈpæti.oʊ/，booth 读作 /buːθ/，以及 fully booked 的连读：FUL-ly booked。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。说预订时突出 re-ser-VA-tion，说露台时把 patio 读成 PAT-ee-oh，说卡座时把 booth 读成 booth。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Eating out

Do you often eat at restaurants? Why or why not?

参考回答（入门训练版）

I eat at restaurants once or twice a week, usually on weekends. It's a nice way to catch up with friends, and I don't have to cook. On busy weekdays I prefer to eat at home because it is cheaper and faster.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A restaurant reservation

Describe a time you made a reservation at a restaurant. Say why you booked the table, how you made the reservation, and how it went.

参考回答（入门训练版）

Last month I called a restaurant to book a table for my friend's birthday. I wanted seven-thirty, but that time was fully booked, so I took an indoor table at eight. I asked for a quieter corner booth and brought a small cake. The dinner went really well, and my friend was happy.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Reservations and dining

Why do some people prefer to reserve a table instead of walking in?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Reserving a table can save time, especially on weekends when popular restaurants get busy. People may also want a special seat, such as a quiet booth for a birthday. Some diners still walk in if they are flexible or if the restaurant usually has empty tables.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习打电话订位：说明人数和日期、想要的时间、室内还是露台，以及有没有特殊安排。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意人数、日期、时间、座位和特殊安排。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ②、④、⑦、⑧、⑪ 句的预订、人数、订满改期、室内座位和留位表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Maya 和 Drew，把人数、时间和座位换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你会怎么预订餐厅：人数和日期、时间、座位偏好，以及是否需要保留座位或自带蛋糕。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

How many people is the table for, and which day?

What time would you like, and is that time available?

Would you prefer indoor or patio seating?

Do you need a quieter table, or would you like to bring a cake?
