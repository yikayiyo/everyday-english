A Quiet Visit to the Aquarium

安静地参观水族馆

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Hannah 和弟弟 Eli 在水族馆里规划参观路线，找到较安静的观赏区。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目是原创口语练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Hannah · 01

The aquarium map shows a jellyfish room upstairs. Want to see it?

地图上说楼上有水母展厅。想去看看吗？

Eli · 02

Yes, but the entrance area is pretty noisy. Can we find a quieter route?

想，不过入口这里挺吵的。我们能找条安静点的路线吗？

Hannah · 03

The staff member said the side hallway is usually calmer.

工作人员说侧面的走廊通常更安静。

Eli · 04

Let's try that. Is there an elevator nearby?

那我们走那里吧。附近有电梯吗？

Hannah · 05

It's just past the large fish tank on our left.

过了左边那个大鱼缸就是。

Eli · 06

I can see it. What else is on the upper floor?

我看到了。楼上还有什么？

Hannah · 07

There's a room where we can watch sea turtles through a window.

有一个房间，我们可以隔着窗户观察海龟。

Eli · 08

I'd like that. Can we stop there before the jellyfish?

我想看。去看水母之前能先在那里停一下吗？

Hannah · 09

Sure. We can sit on the bench and watch for a while.

当然可以。我们可以坐在长椅上看一会儿。

Eli · 10

Great. I brought my notebook to draw what I see.

太好了。我带了笔记本，想把看到的画下来。

Hannah · 11

Then take your time. We don't have to see everything today.

那你慢慢画。我们今天不必看完所有东西。

Eli · 12

Thank you. Those turtles glide through the water so slowly and peacefully.

谢谢你。那些海龟在水中缓缓游动，真宁静。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① a quieter route

一条更安静的路线。

例句：

Is there a quieter route to the exit?

② just past

刚过……的地方。

例句：

The restroom is just past the stairs.

③ for a while

一会儿，一段时间。

例句：

Let's sit here for a while.

④ take your time

慢慢来，不着急。

例句：

Take your time with your drawing.

⑤ don't have to

不必做某事。

例句：

We don't have to leave yet.

03 · American Pronunciation

aquarium 的重音在第二音节；turtle 的重音在第一个音节，两个 t 之间的元音要清楚。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Animals

Do you like watching animals?

Yes. I especially like watching fish because their movement is calming and interesting.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A place where you saw animals

Describe a place where you saw animals. Say where it was, what you saw, and what you remember about it.

I visited an aquarium with my younger brother last year. We took a quiet hallway to the upper floor and found a window overlooking a turtle tank. We sat on a bench and watched the turtles swim slowly past us. My brother drew one in his notebook while I read the information nearby. I remember the visit because we took our time and enjoyed a peaceful afternoon together.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Learning about animals

How can places that display animals help visitors learn?

They can provide clear information about animals and their habitats. Staff can also answer questions and encourage visitors to think about how human choices affect nature.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读后询问较安静的路线，并提议先去看一种动物。
