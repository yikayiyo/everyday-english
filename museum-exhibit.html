SPEAKING LESSON · 23

Finding a Favorite Museum Exhibit

寻找喜欢的博物馆展览

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Ava 和 Noah 在博物馆选择参观顺序，并安排会合地点。本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Ava · 01

The museum has an exhibit about old trains. Want to see it?

博物馆有个关于老式火车的展览。想看吗？

Noah · 02

Definitely. I've always liked trains. Where is it?

当然。我一直喜欢火车。展览在哪里？

Ava · 03

The map says it's on the second floor, near the stairs.

地图上说在二楼，靠近楼梯。

Noah · 04

Great. I also want to see the model town downstairs.

太好了。我还想看楼下的城镇模型。

Ava · 05

We could go there afterward. How much time do we have?

之后可以去那里。我们有多少时间？

Noah · 06

About an hour before we need to leave for dinner.

吃晚饭前大约还有一个小时。

Ava · 07

Then let's spend half an hour with the trains.

那我们花半小时看火车吧。

Noah · 08

That works. Can we take pictures in this exhibit?

可以。这个展览能拍照吗？

Ava · 09

The sign says photos are fine without flash.

指示牌上说可以拍照，但不能用闪光灯。

Noah · 10

I'll turn mine off. That blue train looks interesting.

我会把闪光灯关掉。那辆蓝色火车看起来很有意思。

Ava · 11

You can take a closer look. I want to read this label first.

你可以走近看看。我想先读一下这块说明牌。

Noah · 12

Sure. Let's meet by the stairs in ten minutes.

好。我们十分钟后在楼梯旁碰面。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Want to see it?

想看吗？是口语中省略 Do you 的问法。

例句：

There's a short film. Want to see it?

② afterward

之后，表示接下来发生。

例句：

We had coffee afterward.

③ That works.

可以，表示同意安排。

例句：

Noon? That works for me.

④ without flash

不使用闪光灯。

例句：

Please take the picture without flash.

⑤ take a closer look

走近或更仔细地看。

例句：

Take a closer look at the painting.

03 · American Pronunciation

museum 的重音在第二音节；closer look 中两个词的首音节都较突出。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Museums

What do you like to see in a museum?

I enjoy seeing old objects from everyday life. They help me imagine how people lived in the past.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · An interesting exhibit

Describe an exhibit you enjoyed. Say where it was, what you saw, and why you remember it.

I visited a small museum with my cousin last year. There was an exhibit about trains, and we saw a model of an old station. I liked the little buildings and the tiny people on the platform. We read a few labels and learned how passengers traveled in the past. My cousin took some photos without flash. I remember it because the model made the history easy to imagine.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Exhibits

How can museums make exhibits interesting for different visitors?

They can use clear labels, models, and short recordings. Visitors have different interests and ways of learning, so several ways to explore a subject can help more people enjoy it.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读后与朋友商量参观顺序、剩余时间和会合地点。

08 · 今日口头回答练习
