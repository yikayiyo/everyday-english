SPEAKING LESSON · 19

Reporting a Lost Phone

在火车站失物招领处挂失手机

A2 → B115–20 分钟出行 · 失物招领

Maya 下班后发现手机落在通勤火车上。她赶到联合车站的失物招领处，工作人员 Owen 根据车次、时间和手机外观，在今早的登记里找到一部刚被上交的黑色蓝壳手机。Maya 认出锁屏壁纸是她的狗在海边的照片，出示驾照并在领取表上签字后取回了手机，今天没有手续费。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Owen · 01

Hi there. How can I help you today?

你好。今天有什么可以帮你的？

Maya · 02

Hi. I think I left my phone on the train, and I was hoping someone turned it in.

你好。我好像把手机落在火车上了，希望已经有人交过来了。

Owen · 03

I'm sorry to hear that. Which train were you on, and about what time did you get off?

听到这个我很遗憾。你坐的是哪趟车，大概什么时候下车的？

Maya · 04

I was on the 8:15 into Union Station, and I got off around 8:40. It's a black phone with a blue case.

我坐的是 8:15 到联合车站的那趟，大概 8:40 下的车。是一部黑色手机，配蓝色手机壳。

Owen · 05

Okay. Let me check this morning's log. A commuter turned in a black phone with a blue case about twenty minutes ago.

好的。我先查一下今早的登记。大约二十分钟前，有位乘客交来一部黑色、蓝色壳的手机。

Maya · 06

That sounds like mine. Is there a way I can confirm it's mine?

听起来像我的。有办法确认是我的吗？

Owen · 07

I can show you the lock screen through the bag. If you recognize the wallpaper, I'll have you sign for it.

我可以隔着袋子让你看锁屏。如果你能认出壁纸，就请你签字领取。

Maya · 08

Yes, that's it. The wallpaper is a photo of my dog at the beach.

对，就是它。壁纸是我的狗在海边的照片。

Owen · 09

Perfect. I'll need to see a photo ID, and then you can sign this claim form.

太好了。我需要看一下带照片的证件，然后请你签一下这张领取表。

Maya · 10

Here's my driver's license. Is there a fee to pick it up?

这是我的驾照。取回手机需要收费吗？

Owen · 11

There's no fee today. Just sign here, and please keep an eye on your belongings on the train next time.

今天不收费。在这里签字就行。下次在火车上请看好自己的随身物品。

Maya · 12

I will. Thank you so much for holding on to it. I really thought it was gone.

我会的。非常感谢你们帮我保管。我真以为再也找不回来了。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Turn something in

上交（捡到的失物）。在失物招领处，常说 someone turned it in，表示有人把捡到的东西交了过来。

例句：

I think I left my phone on the train, and I was hoping someone turned it in.

② Get off

下车。上车是 get on，下车是 get off，后面常接 the train，也可以说出大概时间。

例句：

Which train were you on, and about what time did you get off?

③ Sign for it

签字领取。取回失物时，工作人员通常会让你签字确认这件东西是你领走的。

例句：

If you recognize the wallpaper, I'll have you sign for it.

④ Claim form

领取表。取失物时要签署的表格，用来记录是谁领走了物品。

例句：

I'll need to see a photo ID, and then you can sign this claim form.

⑤ Hold on to something

暂为保管、先留着。对失物招领处帮忙保管物品表示感谢时很常用。

例句：

Thank you so much for holding on to it.

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 Union 的开头是 /j/，读作 YOON-yun /ˈjuːnjən/。commuter 的重音在第二音节，读作 kuh-MYOO-ter /kəˈmjuːtər/。wallpaper 的重音在第一音节，读作 WALL-pay-per /ˈwɔːlpeɪpər/。belongings 的重音在第二音节，读作 bi-LONG-ingz /bɪˈlɔːŋɪŋz/。license 读作 LY-suhns /ˈlaɪsns/。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。说车站名时把 Union 的开头读成 /j/，即 YOON-yun；说通勤者时把重音放在第二音节 kuh-MYOO-ter；说壁纸时重音在第一音节 WALL-pay-per；说随身物品时重音在第二音节 bi-LONG-ingz；说驾照时读成 LY-suhns。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Lost things

Have you ever lost your phone or wallet? What do you do if you lose something important?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Yes, I once left my phone on the train. I went to the lost and found as soon as I could and described it, including the case and the lock-screen wallpaper. Luckily, someone had turned it in, so I showed my ID and signed a claim form. Now I try to keep a closer eye on my belongings in public places.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A time you lost something important

Describe a time you lost something important. Say what you lost, where you were, what you did to find it, and how it ended.

参考回答（入门训练版）

I lost my phone on the 8:15 train into Union Station. When I got off around 8:40, I realized it was gone, so I went straight to the lost and found. I told the staff the train time and said it was a black phone with a blue case. They had a phone that someone had turned in, and I recognized the wallpaper of my dog at the beach. I showed my driver's license, signed the claim form, and got it back with no fee.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Lost property in public places

Why do people often lose things on trains or in stations, and how can public places help them get those things back?

参考回答（入门训练版）

People often lose things on trains because they are in a hurry, or they are carrying bags and getting off quickly. Stations can help by having a clear lost and found office, keeping a log of items, and asking for a description or photo ID before giving something back. It also helps when other passengers turn things in instead of leaving them on a seat.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习在失物招领处说明情况：东西掉在哪趟车上、什么时候下车、物品长什么样，以及如何确认、出示证件和签字领取。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意车次和下车时间、手机的颜色和外壳、锁屏壁纸、证件和领取表，以及是否收费。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ②、④、⑤、⑦、⑨、⑪ 句的上交失物、车次时间、确认方式、签字领取和看管随身物品表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Owen 和 Maya，把丢失的物品、车次时间、外观特征和确认方式换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 4–6 句说明你丢失的物品，并询问是否已有人上交、如何确认是你的、要不要证件和手续费。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

Which train were you on, and about what time did you get off?

What does the item look like, and is there a way I can confirm it is mine?

Do I need to show a photo ID and sign a claim form?

Is there a fee to pick it up?
