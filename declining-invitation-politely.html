Declining an Invitation

礼貌拒绝邀请

A2 → B115–20 分钟补充课程 · 2026-11-22

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听；尚未进行浏览器播放或卡拉OK交互验证。零时长词 2 个，可能无法高亮。

Leo 邀请朋友 Maya 周六共进晚餐。Maya 感谢邀请并明确婉拒，不需要详细解释；Leo 尊重她的决定。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Leo · 01

Maya, would you like to join us for dinner on Saturday?

Maya，周六你愿意和我们一起吃晚饭吗？

Maya · 02

Thanks for inviting me, Leo, but I cannot make it.

谢谢你邀请我，Leo，不过我去不了。

Leo · 03

I understand. We will miss you.

我明白。我们会想念你的。

Maya · 04

That is kind of you. I need a quiet evening at home.

你真贴心。我需要在家安静地度过一个晚上。

Leo · 05

Of course. I hope you have a restful evening.

当然。希望你度过一个放松的晚上。

Maya · 06

Thank you. Who else is coming to dinner?

谢谢。还有谁会来吃晚饭？

Leo · 07

A few friends from our art class are coming.

我们美术课上的几个朋友会来。

Maya · 08

Please say hello to them for me.

请代我向他们问好。

Leo · 09

I will. We are trying a new restaurant downtown.

我会的。我们要去市中心尝试一家新餐厅。

Maya · 10

That sounds nice. I hope you all enjoy it.

听起来不错。希望你们玩得开心。

Leo · 11

Thanks. I will tell you about it next week.

谢谢。我下周会告诉你情况。

Maya · 12

I would like that. Have a lovely dinner!

我很乐意听。祝你们晚餐愉快！

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Thanks for inviting me

谢谢你邀请我，用于表达感谢。

例句：

Thanks for inviting me to dinner.

② I cannot make it

我去不了，清楚而礼貌地拒绝邀请。

例句：

I cannot make it on Saturday.

③ a quiet evening

一个安静的夜晚，可简短说明自己的需要。

例句：

I need a quiet evening at home.

④ say hello to them for me

代我向他们问好。

例句：

Please say hello to your friends for me.

⑤ Have a lovely dinner

祝晚餐愉快，用于友好结束谈话。

例句：

Have a lovely dinner tonight.

03 · American Pronunciation

inviting 重音在第二音节 /ɪnˈvaɪtɪŋ/；Saturday 重音在第一音节 /ˈsætərdeɪ/。说 cannot make it 时突出 cannot，使拒绝清楚。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Invitations

How do you respond when a friend invites you somewhere?

I thank my friend and check whether I am free. If I cannot go, I say so clearly and kindly.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · An invitation

Describe an invitation you could not accept. Say who invited you, what it was for, and how you responded.

A friend once invited me to a weekend concert. I was tired after a busy week and wanted to stay home, so I thanked her and said I could not make it. I did not give a long explanation. She understood and went with other friends. The next week she told me about the music, and I was glad she had enjoyed the evening.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Personal boundaries

Why can it be useful to decline an invitation clearly?

A clear answer helps the other person plan. It can also protect your time while keeping the conversation respectful.
