SPEAKING LESSON · 07

Renting a Shared Bike

租一辆共享单车

A2 → B115–20 分钟交通 · 共享出行

Chloe 第一次用共享单车 App，向朋友 Noah 请教怎么扫码解锁、计费方式和还车地点。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Chloe · 01

Hey, can you show me how to rent one of these shared bikes?

嘿，你能教我怎么租这些共享单车吗？

Noah · 02

Sure. Open the app, then scan the QR code on the handlebar.

没问题。打开 App，然后扫描车把上的二维码。

Chloe · 03

Got it. Do I need to unlock anything else after scanning?

明白了。扫完码之后还要再解锁别的东西吗？

Noah · 04

The lock opens automatically. Just wait for the green light and pull the bike out.

锁会自动打开。等绿灯亮了，把车拉出来就行。

Chloe · 05

Nice. How much does it usually cost for a short ride?

不错。短途一般要花多少钱？

Noah · 06

The first thirty minutes are cheap. After that, it charges by the minute.

前三十分钟很便宜。之后就按分钟计费。

Chloe · 07

Okay. Where should I leave it when I'm done?

好。骑完之后应该停在哪里？

Noah · 08

Park it in a marked bike zone. The app will show nearby drop-off spots.

停在标好的停车区。App 会显示附近的还车点。

Chloe · 09

What if there's no free space left in that zone?

要是那个停车区已经没有空位了怎么办？

Noah · 10

Then find the next zone. Don't leave it on the sidewalk, or you'll get a fee.

那就找下一个停车区。别停在人行道上，否则会被收费。

Chloe · 11

Thanks. Should I wear a helmet, or is that optional?

谢谢。我需要戴头盔吗，还是可戴可不戴？

Noah · 12

It's optional here, but I always wear one. Safety first on busy streets.

这里可以不戴，但我每次都会戴。在繁忙街道上安全第一。

听力理解

不看中文，再听一遍对话，尝试回答：

How does Chloe unlock the bike, and where should she leave it?

Chloe 怎么解锁单车？她应该把车停在哪里？

查看答案

她用 App 扫描车把上的二维码，等绿灯亮后把车拉出。还车要停在标好的停车区，不能停在人行道上。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Can you show me how to…?

你能教我怎么……吗？礼貌地请人演示步骤。

例句：

Can you show me how to unlock the bike?

② Scan the QR code

扫描二维码。共享单车、支付和入场常用。

例句：

Scan the QR code on the handlebar to start.

③ It charges by the minute

按分钟计费。说明计费方式。

例句：

After the free time, it charges by the minute.

④ Drop-off spots

还车点/归还点。共享出行里指指定停放处。

例句：

The app shows nearby drop-off spots.

⑤ Safety first

安全第一。提醒优先考虑安全。

例句：

Wear a helmet—safety first.

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 shared bike 的连读，重音落在 BIKE。注意 QR code 读作 /ˌkjuːˈɑːr koʊd/，以及 thirty minutes 的弱读连读。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。请人演示时突出 show me HOW，谈计费时突出 by the MINute，谈还车时突出 DROP-off spots。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Transport

Do you often ride a bike in your city?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Yes, sometimes. I use shared bikes for short trips when the weather is nice. It's faster than walking and cheaper than a taxi.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A form of transport

Describe a form of transport you use in daily life. Say what it is, when you use it, and why you like or dislike it.

参考回答（入门训练版）

I sometimes use shared bikes near my home. I unlock them with an app and ride to the subway station. I like them because they are flexible, but I dislike rainy days.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Shared mobility

What are the advantages and disadvantages of shared bikes in cities?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Shared bikes can reduce traffic and make short trips easy. They are also cheaper than taxis. On the other hand, messy parking can block sidewalks, and bikes may be damaged or hard to find at busy times.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习说明如何扫码解锁、如何计费，以及该把车停在哪里。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意扫码解锁、计费方式、还车点和头盔。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ①、②、⑤、⑧、⑪ 句的询问、扫码和还车表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Chloe 和 Noah，把骑行时间、还车点和是否戴头盔换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你会怎么租车、怎么还车，以及你会不会戴头盔。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

How would you unlock a shared bike?

Where would you leave it when you finish?

Would you wear a helmet? Why or why not?
