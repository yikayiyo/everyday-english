Getting a Haircut

在理发店剪头发

A2 → B115–20 分钟理发店 · 剪发

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听；尚未进行浏览器播放或卡拉OK交互验证。第 4、10 句共有 3 个零时长词（sides、three、cut），可能无法高亮。第 11 句 Whisper 原稿把句首 Perfect 听成了 FACT，对齐后该词仍在，但起始高亮可能偏晚。

Sam 路过街区的理发店，想不预约直接剪头；发型师 Casey 帮他确认修剪幅度、两侧长度和头顶保留，并说明时长、造型和价格。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Sam · 01

Hi, do you take walk-ins? I'd like a haircut today.

你好，你们接受未预约的客人吗？我想今天剪个头。

Casey · 02

We do. I have an opening in about ten minutes. What are you looking for?

接受。大概十分钟后有空位。你想剪成什么样？

Sam · 03

Just a trim, please, and maybe a little shorter on the sides.

稍微修剪一下就好，两侧可以再短一点。

Casey · 04

Sure. How short would you like the sides? I can do a two or a three.

好的。两侧想剪多短？我可以用二号或三号推剪。

Sam · 05

A three, please. And please don't take too much off the top.

请用三号。上面请不要剪掉太多。

Casey · 06

Got it. I'll keep the length on top and just clean it up. Have a seat over here.

明白了。上面会保留长度，只是修整齐。请坐到这边来。

Sam · 07

Thanks. About how long will the haircut take?

谢谢。这次剪发大概需要多长时间？

Casey · 08

About twenty-five minutes. Can I get you some water while I set up?

大概二十五分钟。我准备的时候，要不要给你倒杯水？

Sam · 09

I'm all set, thanks. Will you style my hair at the end?

不用了，谢谢。最后会帮我把头发定型吗？

Casey · 10

Yes. I'll wash, cut, and blow-dry it, so you'll be ready to go.

会的。我会洗、剪，再用吹风机吹干，弄完你就可以直接走。

Sam · 11

Perfect. A little product is fine, too. What's the price for a haircut?

太好了。也可以稍微用一点造型产品。剪发怎么收费？

Casey · 12

It's thirty-two dollars, plus tax. You can pay at the front when we're done.

三十二美元，另加税费。剪完后到前台付款就行。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Do you take walk-ins?

你们接受未预约直接来的客人吗？到店询问是否需要预约时常用。

例句：

Hi, do you take walk-ins? I'd like a haircut today.

② just a trim

稍微修剪一下，表示只剪掉一点，不想改发型。

例句：

Just a trim, please, and maybe a little shorter on the sides.

③ don't take too much off the top

上面不要剪掉太多，说明头顶想保留长度时常用。

例句：

A three, please. And please don't take too much off the top.

④ I'm all set

我不用了 / 我这边没问题，礼貌谢绝对方的额外提议。

例句：

I'm all set, thanks. Will you style my hair at the end?

⑤ plus tax

另加税费，报价时常用来说明标价还没含税。

例句：

It's thirty-two dollars, plus tax. You can pay at the front when we're done.

03 · American Pronunciation

walk-ins 读作 /ˈwɔːk ɪnz/，重音在 walk；trim 的 tr 要读清楚，读作 /trɪm/；sides 读作 /saɪdz/；blow-dry 读作 /ˈbloʊ draɪ/，两个音节都要咬清楚；thirty-two 里的 th 读作 /θ/，不要读成 /s/。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Haircuts

How often do you get a haircut?

I usually get a haircut every five or six weeks. I like a simple style, so I often ask for a trim and a little shorter on the sides. If the salon is busy, I try to go on a weekday afternoon.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Getting a haircut

Describe an imagined time you got a haircut. Say why you went, what you asked the stylist to do, and how the visit went.

Imagine that I walk into a neighborhood salon without an appointment and ask if they take walk-ins. The stylist has an opening in ten minutes, and I ask for a trim, with the sides a little shorter. I also say I do not want too much taken off the top, and the stylist tells me it will take about twenty-five minutes. At the end, the stylist washes, cuts, and blow-dries my hair, and I pay thirty-two dollars plus tax at the front.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Personal style

Why do some people spend a lot of time and money on their hair?

Hair is often part of how people present themselves at work or in social life, so a good haircut can make them feel more confident. Some styles also need regular maintenance, which costs more time and money. Other people prefer a simple, low-maintenance cut because it is cheaper and easier to look after.
