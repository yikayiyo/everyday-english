SPEAKING LESSON · 15

Getting a Phone Plan

在手机店办理套餐

A2 → B115–20 分钟手机 · 套餐

Emma 刚搬到这座城市，走进一家手机店，想用自己已解锁的手机办理月付套餐。店员 Jake 向她介绍流量、热点、是否需要合约，以及能否把原来的号码转过来。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Jake · 01

Hi, welcome in. How can I help you today?

你好，欢迎光临。今天有什么可以帮你的？

Emma · 02

Hi. I just moved here, and I need a phone plan for my own phone.

你好。我刚搬到这里，想给我自己的手机办一个套餐。

Jake · 03

No problem. Is your phone unlocked? If it is, we can set you up with an eSIM today.

没问题。你的手机是解锁的吗？如果是的话，我们今天就可以帮你开通 eSIM。

Emma · 04

Yes, it's unlocked. I'm looking for a monthly plan with enough data for maps and video calls.

是的，已经解锁了。我想办一个月付套餐，流量要够用地图和视频通话。

Jake · 05

The most popular plan is fifty dollars a month. You get unlimited talk and text, plus thirty gigabytes of high-speed data.

最受欢迎的套餐是每月五十美元。包含不限量通话和短信，外加三十吉字节的高速流量。

Emma · 06

What happens if I go over thirty gigabytes?

如果我用超了三十吉字节，会怎么样？

Jake · 07

Your speed would slow down, but you wouldn't be charged extra. You can also add a hotspot for ten dollars more.

网速会变慢，但不会额外收费。你也可以再加十美元开通热点。

Emma · 08

I'll skip the hotspot for now. Do I need to sign a contract?

热点我暂时先不加。我需要签合约吗？

Jake · 09

No contract. You can cancel anytime, but I'd need a photo ID and a credit or debit card.

不用签合约，可以随时取消。不过我需要你的带照片证件，以及一张信用卡或借记卡。

Emma · 10

I have both with me. Can I keep my current number?

这两样我都带了。我能保留现在的号码吗？

Jake · 11

Yes, we can transfer it. It usually takes about twenty minutes to activate.

可以，我们能把号码转过来。一般二十分钟左右就能激活。

Emma · 12

Perfect. Let's go with the fifty-dollar plan and transfer my number.

太好了。那就办这个五十美元的套餐，并把我的号码转过来吧。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Unlocked phone

已解锁的手机。只有解锁后，才可以在别的运营商办理套餐或使用 eSIM。

例句：

Is your phone unlocked? If it is, we can set you up with an eSIM today.

② Unlimited talk and text

不限量通话和短信。套餐说明里常和数据流量分开写。

例句：

You get unlimited talk and text, plus thirty gigabytes of high-speed data.

③ Go over

超出（额度）。这里指用超套餐里的高速流量。

例句：

What happens if I go over thirty gigabytes?

④ No contract

不用签长期合约，通常可以按月付、随时取消。

例句：

No contract. You can cancel anytime, but I'd need a photo ID and a credit or debit card.

⑤ Transfer my number

把现有号码转到新套餐，也常说 keep my number。正式说法是 port my number。

例句：

Let's go with the fifty-dollar plan and transfer my number.

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 gigabytes 的重音：它落在第一音节，读作 GIG-uh-bytes /ˈɡɪɡəbaɪts/。unlimited 的重音在第二音节，读作 un-LIM-i-tid /ʌnˈlɪmɪtɪd/。eSIM 读作 /ˈiːsɪm/。activate 的重音在第一音节，读作 AK-tuh-vayt /ˈæktɪveɪt/。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。说流量单位时把重音放在 GIG-uh-bytes，说不限量时突出 un-LIM-i-tid，开通时把 activate 读成 AK-tuh-vayt。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Phones

How often do you use your phone each day? What do you use it for?

参考回答（入门训练版）

I use my phone a lot, especially for maps, messages, and video calls with my family. I also listen to music on it when I commute. I try not to scroll too much at night, but during the day it's hard to put down.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Getting a phone plan

Describe a time you bought a phone plan or a SIM card. Say why you needed it, what you asked about, and whether you were happy with the plan.

参考回答（入门训练版）

When I moved to a new city, I went to a phone store and asked for a monthly plan for my own unlocked phone. The assistant offered a fifty-dollar plan with unlimited talk and text and thirty gigabytes of data. I asked what would happen if I went over the data, and I skipped the hotspot. I transferred my old number and activated an eSIM that day, which was convenient.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Choosing a phone plan

What do people usually consider when they choose a phone plan?

参考回答（入门训练版）

People usually think about the price, the amount of data, and whether they can keep their number. Many also care if there is a contract, and if the plan works with their own phone. Some people want a hotspot or cheaper roaming, while others just want a simple monthly plan they can cancel anytime.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习在手机店咨询：手机是否已解锁、套餐多少钱、流量用超了怎么办、要不要签合约，以及能不能保留原来的号码。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意手机是否解锁、月付套餐和流量、用超流量会怎样、要不要签合约，以及能不能把原来的号码转过来。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ③、⑤、⑦、⑨、⑫ 句的解锁与 eSIM、五十美元套餐、流量用超、不用合约和转移号码表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Jake 和 Emma，把套餐价格、流量、热点和是否保留原号码换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你会怎么选套餐：你要月付还是预付、需要多少流量，以及会不会把旧号码转过来。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

Is your phone unlocked, and can you use an eSIM?

How much data do you need each month, and what happens if you go over?

Do you need to sign a contract, and what ID or card would you need?

Can you keep or transfer your current number?
