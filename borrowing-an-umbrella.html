SPEAKING LESSON · 62

An Umbrella for the Walk Home

借伞走回家

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：2 个。

办公室同事 Nina 发现下雨没带伞，向同事 Carl 借伞并约定归还。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，内容和练习题均为原创，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Nina · 01

The rain is really coming down, and I left my umbrella at home.

雨下得真大，而我把伞忘在家里了。

Carl · 02

I have a spare one in my desk. Would you like to borrow it?

我桌子里有一把备用的。你想借用吗？

Nina · 03

That would be great. Are you sure you won't need it?

那太好了。你确定自己不需要吗？

Carl · 04

I'm getting a ride home, so I won't need it today.

有人开车送我回家，所以我今天用不上。

Nina · 05

Thank you. I'll bring it back to the office tomorrow.

谢谢。我明天把它带回办公室。

Carl · 06

No problem. It's the blue one with the wooden handle.

没问题。就是那把木柄蓝伞。

Nina · 07

I see it. Does it fold up?

我看到了。它能折叠吗？

Carl · 08

Yes. Press the button on the handle to close it.

能。按手柄上的按钮就能收起来。

Nina · 09

Got it. I'll let it dry before I bring it back.

明白了。我会晾干之后再带回来。

Carl · 10

Thanks. You can leave it on my desk if I'm in a meeting.

谢谢。如果我在开会，你可以把它放在我桌上。

Nina · 11

I will. I hope your ride gets here soon.

好的。希望接你的人快点到。

Carl · 12

Thanks, and have a safe walk home.

谢谢，你走回家路上小心。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① come down

形容雨或雪下得大。

例句：

The snow was coming down when we left.

② a spare

一个备用物品。

例句：

I keep a spare in my bag.

③ get a ride

搭便车或有人开车接送。

例句：

Can you get a ride to the station?

④ fold up

折叠起来。

例句：

This chair folds up for easy storage.

⑤ let it dry

让它晾干。

例句：

Let the towel dry near the window.

03 · American Pronunciation

umbrella 重音在第二音节 BREL；spare 开头的 /sp/ 要连在一起，不要在两个辅音中间加元音。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Rainy days

What do you usually carry on a rainy day?

I usually take a small umbrella and a bag that can keep my books dry. I also check the weather before leaving home.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Help on a rainy day

Describe a time someone helped you on a rainy day. Say where you were, what happened, and how you thanked them.

One afternoon I left work and saw heavy rain outside. I had forgotten my umbrella, and I had a long walk home. A coworker offered me a spare umbrella because she was getting a ride. I thanked her and promised to return it the next day. At home I left it open to dry, and the next morning I put it on her desk with a short thank you note.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Small acts of help

Why can small acts of help matter at work?

They can make a difficult moment easier and show that coworkers notice one another. Over time, such kindness may make people feel more comfortable asking for help.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读借伞的对话，再说明忘带伞的情况并约定归还时间。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

I forgot my umbrella, and it's raining hard. Could I borrow your spare one? I'll bring it back tomorrow.
