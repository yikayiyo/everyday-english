SPEAKING LESSON · 59

Sorting Family Photos

整理家庭照片

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Grace 和弟弟 Ryan 整理家里的旧纸质照片，决定如何分类并保存说明。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，题目是原创口语练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Grace · 01

I found a box of family photos in the closet. Want to sort them with me?

我在壁橱里找到一盒家庭照片。想和我一起整理吗？

Ryan · 02

I'd love to. Some of these pictures are older than I am.

我很乐意。有些照片比我的年纪还大。

Grace · 03

Let's start by putting the photos from each trip together.

我们先把每次旅行的照片分别放在一起吧。

Ryan · 04

Good idea. Where does this beach picture belong?

好主意。这张海滩照片应该放在哪里？

Grace · 05

I think it was taken on our trip to Maine. Mom might remember.

我想这是我们去缅因州旅行时拍的。妈妈可能记得。

Ryan · 06

I'll put it in a separate pile until we ask her.

问她之前，我先把它放在单独的一摞里。

Grace · 07

Could you write the places and dates on these cards?

你能把地点和日期写在这些卡片上吗？

Ryan · 08

Sure. I'll leave a blank when we aren't certain.

当然。不确定的地方我会留空。

Grace · 09

Then we can put each group in an envelope with its card.

然后我们可以把每组照片和卡片一起放进信封。

Ryan · 10

Should we make copies of our favorites to share with the family?

我们要不要把最喜欢的照片复制一份，和家人分享？

Grace · 11

Yes. Let's choose a few once we've finished sorting.

好。等整理完后我们挑几张。

Ryan · 12

Deal. I'll ask Mom about the beach photo this evening.

说定了。我今晚问问妈妈那张海滩照片。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① sort photos

整理、分类照片。

例句：

I spent an hour sorting old photos.

② belong

属于某处；放在合适位置。

例句：

Does this photo belong in that folder?

③ a separate pile

单独的一摞。

例句：

Put the letters in a separate pile.

④ leave a blank

留空。

例句：

Leave a blank if you don't know the date.

⑤ make copies

制作副本。

例句：

I'll make copies of these pictures.

03 · American Pronunciation

photo 重读第一个音节；在 old photos 中注意 old 末尾的 /d/ 和 photos 开头的 /f/ 分属两个词。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Photos

Do you look at old photos often?

Sometimes. I like looking at family photos because they remind me of places and people I love.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A photograph you like

Describe a photograph you like. Say what is in it, when it was taken, and why it matters to you.

One of my favorite photographs shows my family standing on a beach. It was taken during a summer trip when I was a child. My brother is holding a small shell, and everyone is smiling at the camera. I found the picture again while sorting a box with him. It matters to me because it reminds me of a relaxed day we spent together.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Keeping memories

Why do people keep photographs even when they remember an event?

Photos can bring back details that fade over time, such as a place or a person's expression. They also let people share memories with relatives who were not there.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读后提议怎样给照片分类，说明不确定日期时怎么办。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

Let's put the trip photos together. I'll write the places on cards and leave a blank if we don't know the date. Then we can choose a few to share.
