SPEAKING LESSON · 08

Mailing a Package at the Post Office

在邮局寄包裹

A2 → B115–20 分钟邮局 · 寄件

Nina 第一次去邮局给国外的朋友寄生日礼物，向柜员 Owen 询问包装要求、运输方式和运费。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Nina · 01

Hi. I'd like to mail this package to a friend in Canada.

你好。我想把这个包裹寄给加拿大的一位朋友。

Owen · 02

Sure. Is there anything fragile or liquid inside?

没问题。里面有易碎品或者液体吗？

Nina · 03

Just a small ceramic mug and a card. No liquids.

只有一个小陶瓷杯子和一张卡片。没有液体。

Owen · 04

Okay. Wrap the mug in bubble wrap, then seal the box with packing tape.

好。请先用气泡膜包好杯子，再用胶带把箱子封好。

Nina · 05

Got it. How long will regular shipping take?

明白了。普通邮寄大概要多久？

Owen · 06

About seven to ten business days. Express usually takes three to five.

大概七到十个工作日。快递一般三到五天。

Nina · 07

I'll take regular shipping. Do I need to fill out a customs form?

我选普通邮寄。需要填海关申报单吗？

Owen · 08

Yes. Write the contents and approximate value on this form.

需要。在这张表格上写明物品内容和大概价值。

Nina · 09

Okay. How much will it cost for this size?

好。这个尺寸大概要多少钱？

Owen · 10

Eighteen dollars, including tracking. Would you like insurance as well?

十八美元，含物流追踪。要不要顺便加保险？

Nina · 11

Yes, please add basic insurance. Can I pay by card?

好，请加基础保险。可以刷卡吗？

Owen · 12

Of course. Here's your receipt and tracking number. Have a nice day!

当然可以。这是您的收据和运单号。祝您今天愉快！

听力理解

不看中文，再听一遍对话，尝试回答：

What is Nina mailing, and which shipping option does she choose?

Nina 要寄什么？她选了哪种运输方式？

查看答案

她给加拿大的朋友寄一个陶瓷杯子和一张卡片。她选了普通邮寄，并加了基础保险。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① I'd like to mail this package

我想寄这个包裹。邮局、快递柜常用开场。

例句：

I'd like to mail this package overseas.

② Anything fragile inside?

里面有易碎品吗？柜员核对寄件内容时常用。

例句：

Is there anything fragile or liquid inside?

③ Business days

工作日。说时效时通常不含周末。

例句：

It takes seven to ten business days.

④ Fill out a customs form

填写海关申报单。国际寄件必备。

例句：

You'll need to fill out a customs form.

⑤ Tracking number

运单号/物流追踪号。查询包裹进度用。

例句：

Keep your tracking number to check the delivery.

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 package 的重音在 PAC。注意 customs form 连读时 form 清晰送气，business days 里 business 的弱读 /ˈbɪznəs/，以及 tracking number 的节奏：TRACKing NUMber。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。寄件开场突出 mail this PACKage，谈时效时突出 business DAYS，拿收据时突出 TRACKing number。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Letters and packages

Do you often send letters or packages?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Not very often. I mostly send packages for birthdays or holidays. For everyday messages, I just use my phone.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A time you sent something

Describe a time you sent a letter or a package. Say what you sent, where it went, and how you felt about it.

参考回答（入门训练版）

Last year I mailed a birthday gift to my friend in Canada. I packed a mug carefully and chose regular shipping. I felt happy when she received it safely.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Online shopping and delivery

How has online shopping changed the way people send and receive packages?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Online shopping has made delivery much more common. People expect fast shipping and tracking. On the other hand, more packages also mean more packaging waste and busy post offices.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习说明包裹里有什么、选哪种运输，以及是否需要保险。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意包装要求、运输时效、海关表格和保险。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ①、④、⑦、⑩、⑫ 句的寄件、包装和付款表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Nina 和 Owen，把目的地、包裹内容和运输方式换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你会怎么寄一个国际包裹：内容、包装、运输方式和付款。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

What would you put inside the package?

Would you choose regular or express shipping? Why?

Would you add insurance?
