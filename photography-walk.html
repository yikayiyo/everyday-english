A Walk to Photograph the City

城市摄影散步

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

Clara 和 Jay 在旧街区约定拍摄主题，并尊重路人的意愿。 本课为 2026-11-22 预制系列原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Clara · 01

Would you like to take a photography walk this Saturday morning?

这周六早上你想一起去摄影散步吗？

Jay · 02

Yes. Do you have a place in mind?

想啊。你想好去哪里了吗？

Clara · 03

The old streets near the river have interesting doors and signs.

河边的老街上有有趣的门和招牌。

Jay · 04

That sounds fun. Should we pick a theme for our pictures?

听起来有趣。我们要给照片选个主题吗？

Clara · 05

How about colors? We could each look for three bright details.

颜色怎么样？我们可以各自找三个鲜艳的细节。

Jay · 06

I like that. I will bring my camera, but my phone works too.

我喜欢。我会带相机，不过手机也可以拍。

Clara · 07

I will use my phone. What if someone is in the picture?

我会用手机。如果有人出现在画面里怎么办？

Jay · 08

We can ask before taking a close photo, and respect a no.

拍近照前可以先问，并尊重对方的拒绝。

Clara · 09

Agreed. Where should we meet?

同意。我们在哪里见？

Jay · 10

At the bridge entrance at nine. We can walk from there.

九点在桥的入口处见。我们可以从那里出发。

Clara · 11

Afterward, could we compare our favorite shots over coffee?

之后我们能边喝咖啡边比较最喜欢的照片吗？

Jay · 12

Definitely. I am curious to see what catches your eye.

当然。我很好奇什么会吸引你的目光。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① have a place in mind

心里有想去的地方。

例句：

Do you have a restaurant in mind?

② pick a theme

选择主题。

例句：

Let us pick a theme for the display.

③ bright details

鲜艳的细节。

例句：

The artist added bright details to the painting.

④ favorite shot

最喜欢的一张照片；shot 指照片。

例句：

This is my favorite shot from the trip.

⑤ catch your eye

吸引你的注意。

例句：

The red door caught my eye.

03 · American Pronunciation

photography 的重音在第二音节；picture 的重音在第一音节，结尾是轻读音节。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Photos

What do you like taking photos of?

I like taking photos of small details, such as colorful doors and street signs. They help me remember a place.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A photo walk

Describe a walk where you took photos. Say where you went, what you photographed, and why you enjoyed it.

I went for a photo walk with a friend near the river. We chose colors as our theme and looked for bright doors, signs, and flowers along the old streets. I used my phone, while my friend brought a camera. Later we compared our favorite pictures over coffee. It was interesting to see how differently we noticed the same neighborhood.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Photography

How can taking photos change the way people notice a place?

Looking for a photo can make people pay attention to details they usually pass by. However, it also helps to put the camera away sometimes and enjoy the place directly.

08 · 今日口头回答练习
