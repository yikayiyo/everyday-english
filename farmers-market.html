SPEAKING LESSON · 05

Shopping at a Farmers Market

在农夫市场买菜

A2 → B115–20 分钟购物 · 市场

Maya 周末去农夫市场买新鲜食材，向摊主 Chris 打听西红柿和草莓的情况。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Maya · 01

Hi! Are these tomatoes grown nearby?

你好！这些西红柿是附近种的吗？

Chris · 02

They are. We picked them this morning on our farm.

是的。我们今天早上刚从自家农场摘下来。

Maya · 03

They look really fresh. How much are they?

看起来真新鲜。怎么卖？

Chris · 04

Three dollars a pound, or five for two pounds.

一磅三美元，两磅五美元。

Maya · 05

I'll take two pounds. Do you have strawberries as well?

我要两磅。你们也有草莓吗？

Chris · 06

Yes, but they're almost sold out. There's one box left.

有，不过快卖完了。只剩一盒了。

Maya · 07

Perfect. I've been meaning to make a fruit salad.

太好了。我一直想做一份水果沙拉。

Chris · 08

Then these should be a good fit. They're sweet and ripe.

那这盒应该挺适合你。又甜又熟。

Maya · 09

Great. Can I pay with a card, or do you take cash only?

好。我能刷卡吗，还是只收现金？

Chris · 10

Either works. There's a small card fee of fifty cents.

都行。刷卡会多收五十美分手续费。

Maya · 11

Cash is fine. Thanks for the tip about the strawberries.

那就付现金吧。谢谢你提醒草莓快卖完了。

Chris · 12

Anytime. Enjoy your weekend cooking!

不客气。祝你周末做饭愉快！

听力理解

不看中文，再听一遍对话，尝试回答：

What does Maya buy, and why does she choose to pay cash?

Maya 买了什么？她为什么选择付现金？

查看答案

她买了两磅西红柿和最后一盒草莓。付现金可以避免五十美分的刷卡手续费。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① How much are they?

怎么卖？/多少钱？询问价格的自然说法。

例句：

How much are the apples today?

② I've been meaning to…

我一直想做……，但还没真正去做。

例句：

I've been meaning to cook more at home.

③ A good fit

很合适的人或事物。

例句：

These berries are a good fit for a fruit salad.

④ Almost sold out

快卖完了。

例句：

The bread is almost sold out.

⑤ Either works.

两种都可以。用于给出灵活选择。

例句：

We can meet at ten or eleven. Either works.

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 tomatoes 的美式发音 /təˈmeɪtoʊz/，以及 two pounds 的连读。注意 strawberries 的重音在第一音节 STRAW。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。问价时突出 How MUCH are they?，表达数量时突出 TWO POUNDS。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Markets

Do you like going to markets?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Yes, I do. I like farmers markets because the food is fresher. I usually go on weekends with a friend.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A place to buy food

Describe a place where you like to buy food. Say where it is, what you buy there, and why you like it.

参考回答（入门训练版）

I like shopping at a local farmers market. It is outdoors and open on weekends. I usually buy fruit and vegetables there because they taste fresher than supermarket food.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Local food

Why do some people prefer to buy local food?

参考回答（入门训练版）

I think local food is often fresher, and buying it can support nearby farmers. Some people also care about reducing transportation and knowing where their food comes from.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习在市场询问价格并决定买什么。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意产地、价格、数量和付款方式。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ③、⑤、⑦、⑨ 句的问价、购买、计划和付款表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Maya 和 Chris，把西红柿和草莓换成自己想买的食材，练习询问价格、决定数量和选择付款方式。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你想买什么、怎么问价，以及你为什么选择现金或刷卡。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

What would you like to buy at a farmers market?

How would you ask about the price?

Would you pay cash or use a card? Why?
