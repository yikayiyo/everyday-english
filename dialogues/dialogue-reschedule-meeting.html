Rescheduling a meeting with a colleague

和同事改约会议时间

对话 · Dialogue

**Lena

** Hi Tom, have you got a minute? It's about Thursday's project review.

**Tom

** Sure, what's up?

**Lena

** Something's come up with a client, and I won't be able to make the 2 p.m. slot. Would it be possible to push it back to Friday morning?

**Tom

** Friday morning is a bit tight for me, but I could do 11. Does that work for you?

**Lena

** 11 works perfectly. I'll send out an updated invite to the team.

**Tom

** Great. Could you also circulate the slides beforehand so everyone can take a look?

**Lena

** Will do. I'll get them out by Thursday afternoon at the latest.

**Tom

** Perfect. Thanks for the heads-up.

值得学的表达

  1. Something's come up. — 「临时有事」。come up 指事情突然发生，用来委婉说明改期原因。
  2. make (the 2 p.m. slot) — 「赶得上、能参加」；slot = 排好的时间段。
  3. push it back — 把时间往后推、推迟（提前是 bring it forward）。
  4. Does that work for you? — 「这个时间你方便吗」。
  5. Thanks for the heads-up. — 「谢谢你提前告诉我」。heads-up = 提前打的招呼或提醒（口语）。
