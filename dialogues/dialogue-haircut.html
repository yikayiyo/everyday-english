Getting a haircut

在理发店剪头发

对话 · Dialogue

**Stylist

** Hi, come on in. What are we doing today?

**Customer

** Just a trim, please. I'm trying to grow it out, so not too much.

**Stylist

** Sure. How much should I take off? About an inch?

**Customer

** Maybe a little less than that. And could you trim my bangs? They're getting in my eyes.

**Stylist

** No problem. ... All done. How does that look?

**Customer

** Perfect, thanks. That's $35, right? Here's forty. Keep the change.

值得学的表达

  1. just a trim — 只修一修，稍微剪短一点。
  2. grow (something) out — 把头发留长，先不剪。
  3. take (something) off — 剪掉、去掉。理发时 How much should I take off? = 要剪掉多少？
  4. bangs / fringe — 刘海（美式 bangs；英式 fringe）。
  5. Keep the change. — 不用找零了（剩下的当小费）。
