Asking about a gym membership

健身房咨询办会员

对话 · Dialogue

**Staff

** Hi there. Can I help you?

**You

** Hi. I'm interested in joining — do you offer a free trial?

**Staff

** We do. If you sign up today, you get a seven-day free trial with full access to the gym floor and group classes.

**You

** That sounds good. Could you walk me through the membership options after the trial?

**Staff

** Sure. After the trial, memberships are month-to-month. There's no long-term commitment.

**You

** And can I cancel if it isn't a good fit?

**Staff

** Yes. Just give us 14 days' notice and we'll cancel it for you.

值得学的表达

  1. sign up — 报名、注册参加某个活动或组织。
  2. free trial — 免费试用一段时间。
  3. walk someone through (something) — 耐心地一步步讲解或演示怎么做。
  4. month-to-month — 按月续约，没有很长的固定合同期。
  5. (a) good fit — 很合适、匹配度高；取消时说 give … days' notice（提前若干天正式通知）。
