Hotel check-in

酒店前台办理入住

场景对话 · Dialogue

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

6 句连续播放，适合磨耳朵或跟读前先过一遍。

Guest · 01

Hi, I'd like to check in. I have a reservation in the name of Chen.

Receptionist · 02

Good afternoon. Yes, I can see your booking for two nights. I'm afraid your room isn't ready yet — check-in usually starts at three.

Guest · 03

Oh, okay. Would you mind holding my bags while I wait in the lobby?

Receptionist · 04

Of course. We'll store them for you. There's also complimentary coffee over there if you'd like some.

Guest · 05

That's great, thanks. Could I get the keycard as soon as the room is ready?

Receptionist · 06

Absolutely. I'll let you know as soon as it's available.

值得学的表达

  1. check in — 到酒店报到、办理入住。
  2. in the name of someone — 预订登记在某人名下。
  3. I'm afraid... — 委婉说出不好的消息或不同意见。
  4. Would you mind + V-ing — 礼貌请求别人做某事；后面用动名词。
  5. complimentary / keycard — （商家）免费赠送的；电子门卡。
