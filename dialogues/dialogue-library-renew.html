Renewing library books

图书馆续借逾期图书并预约保留

场景对话 · Dialogue

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

6 句连续播放，适合磨耳朵或跟读前先过一遍。

Student · 01

Hi. I'd like to renew these two books, please. Are they overdue?

Librarian · 02

Let me check… Yes, one is three days overdue, so there'll be a small late fee. I can renew both for another two weeks, though.

Student · 03

That's fine. How much is the fine?

Librarian · 04

It's two dollars. Also, did you want me to put this title on reserve for you? Someone just returned a copy.

Student · 05

Yes, please. Can I check it out today?

Librarian · 06

Sure. Here's your receipt — the new due date is November 12th.

值得学的表达

  1. renew (a book / a loan) — 续借；延长可用期限。
  2. overdue — 过期未还／逾期。剑桥例句：*My library books are a week overdue.*
  3. late fee / fine — 滞纳金／罚款。
  4. due date — 到期日／应还日期。
  5. put something on reserve / check out (a book) — 预约保留／从图书馆借出（书）。
