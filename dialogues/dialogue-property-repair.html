Calling property management about a leaky faucet

电话物业报修（厨房水龙头滴水）

对话 · Dialogue

**Resident（住户）

** Hi, this is apartment 1203 in Building 5. My kitchen faucet has been dripping all night. Could someone come take a look?

**Staff（物业）

** Of course. May I have your name and a callback number?

**Resident

** Sure — Li Wei, and my number is 138-0000-1234. What's the earliest slot you have?

**Staff

** We can send a maintenance worker this afternoon around three. Does that work for you?

**Resident

** Three works. Will they call before they come, or should I leave the door unlocked?

**Staff

** We'll call about fifteen minutes ahead. Please make sure someone is home.

值得学的表达

  1. drip / dripping — 液体一滴一滴落下；水龙头「一直在滴」就说 has been dripping。
  2. take a look — 过来看一眼 / 检查一下，口语里很常用。
  3. callback number — 方便对方回电的号码。
  4. (time) slot — 可预约的时段；约维修、约配送都能用。
  5. faucet — 水龙头（美式；英式更常说 tap）。搭配可记 leaky faucet。另：maintenance worker = 维修工人。
