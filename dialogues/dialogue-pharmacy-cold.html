Buying cold medicine at a pharmacy

药店买感冒药

Customer · 01

Hi, I've got a bit of a cold. Could you recommend something for a sore throat and a runny nose?

Pharmacist · 02

Sure. Are you allergic to any medicines?

Customer · 03

Not that I know of. And I'd prefer something that won't make me drowsy — I've got work later.

Pharmacist · 04

In that case, these lozenges should help your throat, and this is a non-drowsy decongestant for the congestion. Take one tablet every six hours with water.

Customer · 05

Great. Do I need a prescription for either of them?

Pharmacist · 06

No, they're both available over the counter. Anything else?

Customer · 07

That's all, thanks. How much is that?

值得学的表达

  1. a runny nose — 流鼻涕（感冒或过敏时常见）。
  2. Not that I know of. — 口语习语，「据我所知没有」。
  3. drowsy / non-drowsy — 昏昏欲睡的／不引起困倦的（药盒常见标注）。
  4. lozenge — 润喉含片。
  5. over the counter — 非处方、可直接在柜台买。
