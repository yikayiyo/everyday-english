Returning faulty shoes

去商店退货（鞋子开胶了）

场景对话 · Dialogue

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

9 句连续播放，适合磨耳朵或跟读前先过一遍。

Clerk · 01

Hi there, how can I help you?

Customer · 02

Hi, I'd like to return these shoes. I bought them two weeks ago, and they're already falling apart.

Clerk · 03

Oh, I'm sorry about that. Do you have the receipt or any other proof of purchase?

Customer · 04

Yes, here's the receipt. Could I get a full refund?

Clerk · 05

Sure. Since they're faulty, you can get a full refund, or you can exchange them for another pair if you prefer.

Customer · 06

Hmm, do you have the same style in a different color?

Clerk · 07

Let me check… We don't, I'm afraid. But if you'd like, I can give you store credit instead, and you can use it anytime.

Customer · 08

Thanks, but I'll just take the refund, please.

Clerk · 09

No problem. It'll go back onto your card in three to five business days.

值得学的表达

  1. fall apart — 散架、坏掉。剑桥例句：*My poor old boots are falling apart.*
  2. proof of purchase — 购买凭证（小票、发票等）。
  3. a full refund — 全额退款。
  4. exchange A for B — 把 A 换成 B（商店换货）。
  5. store credit — 退货后店里给的等值购物额度（美式；英式常说 *credit note*）。
