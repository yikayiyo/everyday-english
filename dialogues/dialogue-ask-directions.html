Asking for directions to a bookstore

问路（车站附近找书店）

场景对话 · Dialogue

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

6 句连续播放，适合磨耳朵或跟读前先过一遍。

A · 01

Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to Greenwood Bookstore? I think it's near here.

B · 02

Sure. Go straight ahead along this road for about two blocks, then take a left at the traffic lights.

A · 03

Okay—two blocks, then left. Is it within walking distance?

B · 04

Yes, it's only about a five-minute walk. After you turn, you'll see a small park on your right. The bookstore is just around the block from there—you can't miss it. There's a big green sign above the door.

A · 05

Got it. Thanks so much!

B · 06

You're welcome. Enjoy your visit.

值得学的表达

  1. Excuse me — 礼貌地引起陌生人注意、开口问路时常用。
  2. take a left — 在下一个路口向左转（mainly US；英式更常说 turn left）。
  3. within walking distance — 走着就能到、距离不远。
  4. (just) around the block — 就在拐过去那条街上 / 很近。
  5. You can't miss it — 很好找、绝对不会错过。
