Booking a dental checkup

打电话预约牙医检查

对话 · Dialogue

**Receptionist

** Good afternoon, Bright Smile Dental. How can I help you?

**You

** Hi, I'd like to make an appointment for a checkup, please. I've had a toothache for a few days.

**Receptionist

** Of course. Are you an existing patient with us?

**You

** Yes — Alex Chen. I last came in about a year ago.

**Receptionist

** Thanks. We're pretty booked up this week, but I have Thursday at 3:30 or Friday at 10. Which works for you?

**You

** Thursday at 3:30 works for me. Do I need to bring anything?

**Receptionist

** Just your ID and insurance card if you have one. We'll see you then.

值得学的表达

  1. make an appointment — 预约（看病、会面等）。剑桥例句：I'd like to make an appointment with Dr. Evans, please.
  2. checkup（英式常写作 check-up）— 健康检查；也可说 dental checkup（牙科检查）。
  3. toothache — 牙痛。剑桥常见说法：I have terrible toothache.
  4. booked up — 已约满 / 没空档（活动、航班、诊所时段等）。
  5. works for me — （某个时间或安排）对我合适 / 行得通。
