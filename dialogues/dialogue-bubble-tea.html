Ordering bubble tea

奶茶店点单（少糖、boba、外带）

对话 · Dialogue

**Staff

** Hi! What can I get for you?

**Customer

** Hi. Could I get a large brown sugar milk tea, please? With boba.

**Staff

** Sure. How sweet would you like it?

**Customer

** Not too sweet, please — half sugar if you have that.

**Staff

** No problem. For here or to go?

**Customer

** To go, please. How long will it take?

**Staff

** About five minutes. That'll be eighteen yuan.

值得学的表达

  1. What can I get for you? — 店员点单时很常用，「您要点什么？」
  2. Could I get …? — 礼貌地点单。
  3. For here or to go? / to go — 「堂食还是外带？」
  4. boba — 珍珠/波霸；也可指整杯奶茶。
  5. That'll be … — 结账时报价的口语说法。
