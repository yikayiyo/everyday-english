Calling about a delayed package

打电话问快递延误

对话 · Dialogue

**Customer

** Hi, I'm calling about a package that was supposed to arrive yesterday. My tracking number is AB123456789.

**Agent

** Of course. Could you bear with me for a moment while I pull that up?

**Customer

** Sure, no problem.

**Agent

** Thanks for holding. It shows your order was out for delivery yesterday, but the driver couldn't leave it. I'll look into what went wrong.

**Customer

** Okay. When can I expect it now?

**Agent

** I'll sort this out and get back to you within the hour with a new delivery window.

值得学的表达

  1. bear with me — 请稍等、耐心等我一下（常用于电话）。
  2. out for delivery — （包裹）已交由派送员、正在派送途中。
  3. look into something — 调查、了解某事的原因。
  4. sort something out — 把问题处理好、解决掉。
  5. get back to someone — 稍后（通常打电话）再联系对方并告知信息。
