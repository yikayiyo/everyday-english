# 今日场景：去商店退货（鞋子开胶了）

**Date:** 2026-10-21
**Slug:** dialogue-return-shoes
**Scene ZH:** 去商店退货（鞋子开胶了）
**Roles:** Clerk / Customer
**Audio:** none

## Dialogue

**Clerk:** Hi there, how can I help you?

**Customer:** Hi, I'd like to return these shoes. I bought them two weeks ago, and they're already falling apart.

**Clerk:** Oh, I'm sorry about that. Do you have the receipt or any other proof of purchase?

**Customer:** Yes, here's the receipt. Could I get a full refund?

**Clerk:** Sure. Since they're faulty, you can get a full refund, or you can exchange them for another pair if you prefer.

**Customer:** Hmm, do you have the same style in a different color?

**Clerk:** Let me check… We don't, I'm afraid. But if you'd like, I can give you store credit instead, and you can use it anytime.

**Customer:** Thanks, but I'll just take the refund, please.

**Clerk:** No problem. It'll go back onto your card in three to five business days.

## 值得学的表达

1. **fall apart** — 散架、坏掉。剑桥例句：*My poor old boots are falling apart.*
2. **proof of purchase** — 购买凭证（小票、发票等）。
3. **a full refund** — 全额退款。
4. **exchange A for B** — 把 A 换成 B（商店换货）。
5. **store credit** — 退货后店里给的等值购物额度（美式；英式常说 *credit note*）。
