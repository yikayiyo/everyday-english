# 今日场景：问路（车站附近找书店）

**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Slug:** dialogue-ask-directions
**Scene ZH:** 问路（车站附近找书店）
**Roles:** A / B
**Audio:** none

## Dialogue

**A:** Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to Greenwood Bookstore? I think it's near here.

**B:** Sure. Go straight ahead along this road for about two blocks, then take a left at the traffic lights.

**A:** Okay—two blocks, then left. Is it within walking distance?

**B:** Yes, it's only about a five-minute walk. After you turn, you'll see a small park on your right. The bookstore is just around the block from there—you can't miss it. There's a big green sign above the door.

**A:** Got it. Thanks so much!

**B:** You're welcome. Enjoy your visit.

## 值得学的表达

1. **Excuse me** — 礼貌地引起陌生人注意、开口问路时常用。
2. **take a left** — 在下一个路口向左转（mainly US；英式更常说 turn left）。
3. **within walking distance** — 走着就能到、距离不远。
4. **(just) around the block** — 就在拐过去那条街上 / 很近。
5. **You can't miss it** — 很好找、绝对不会错过。
