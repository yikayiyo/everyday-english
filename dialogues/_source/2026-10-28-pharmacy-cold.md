# 今日场景：药店买感冒药

**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Slug:** dialogue-pharmacy-cold
**Scene ZH:** 药店买感冒药
**Roles:** Customer / Pharmacist
**Audio:** voice memo in chat (EN lines only)

## Dialogue

**Customer:** Hi, I've got a bit of a cold. Could you recommend something for a sore throat and a runny nose?

**Pharmacist:** Sure. Are you allergic to any medicines?

**Customer:** Not that I know of. And I'd prefer something that won't make me drowsy — I've got work later.

**Pharmacist:** In that case, these lozenges should help your throat, and this is a non-drowsy decongestant for the congestion. Take one tablet every six hours with water.

**Customer:** Great. Do I need a prescription for either of them?

**Pharmacist:** No, they're both available over the counter. Anything else?

**Customer:** That's all, thanks. How much is that?

## 值得学的表达

1. **a runny nose** — 流鼻涕（感冒或过敏时常见）。
2. **Not that I know of.** — 口语习语，「据我所知没有」。
3. **drowsy / non-drowsy** — 昏昏欲睡的／不引起困倦的（药盒常见标注）。
4. **lozenge** — 润喉含片。
5. **over the counter** — 非处方、可直接在柜台买。
