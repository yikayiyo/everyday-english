# 今日场景：超市里找商品

**Date:** 2026-10-27
**Slug:** dialogue-supermarket-aisle
**Scene ZH:** 超市里找商品（橄榄油）
**Roles:** A（顾客）/ B（店员）
**Audio:** none (voice memo started 2026-10-28)

## Dialogue

**A（顾客）:** Excuse me — I'm after some olive oil. Which aisle is that in?

**B（店员）:** Aisle 6, next to the pasta. Want me to show you?

**A:** No need, thanks. One more thing — is the organic brand still on sale?

**B:** The organic one's out of stock today, I'm afraid. But the regular bottles are on special offer: buy one, get one free.

**A:** Perfect. I'll stock up on those then. Thanks for your help.

**B:** You're welcome. Enjoy your shopping.

## 值得学的表达

1. **be after something**（informal）— 想找 / 想买某物。
2. **aisle** — 超市货架之间的过道。
3. **out of stock** — 缺货。
4. **on special offer / on sale** — 特价、促销。
5. **stock up (on something)** — 多买一些存着。
