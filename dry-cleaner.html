SPEAKING LESSON · 09

Dropping Off Clothes at a Dry Cleaner

在干洗店送洗衣服

A2 → B115–20 分钟干洗 · 去渍

Lena 把一件沾了红酒渍的外套送到干洗店，向店员 Chris 询问能否去渍、取件时间和价格。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Lena · 01

Hi. I'd like to drop off this coat for dry cleaning.

你好。我想把这件外套送去干洗。

Chris · 02

Sure. Is there a stain you'd like us to treat?

没问题。有什么污渍需要我们处理吗？

Lena · 03

Yes, there's a red wine stain on the right sleeve.

有的，右袖上有一块红酒渍。

Chris · 04

Got it. We'll pretreat the stain before we clean the coat.

明白了。我们会先预处理污渍，再干洗这件外套。

Lena · 05

Great. How long will it usually take?

太好了。一般要多久能取？

Chris · 06

Regular service is two days. Same-day pickup is available until three.

普通服务两天。当天取件可以，最晚三点前。

Lena · 07

Two days is fine. Do I need to leave a phone number?

两天可以。需要留电话号码吗？

Chris · 08

Yes, please. We'll text you when it's ready for pickup.

需要。衣服好了我们会发短信通知你来取。

Lena · 09

Okay. How much will the coat cost?

好。这件外套大概多少钱？

Chris · 10

Eighteen dollars, including stain treatment.

十八美元，含去渍处理。

Lena · 11

Perfect. Can I pay when I pick it up?

很好。我可以取件的时候再付款吗？

Chris · 12

Of course. Here's your claim ticket. See you in two days!

当然可以。这是您的取件单。两天后见！

听力理解

不看中文，再听一遍对话，尝试回答：

What stain is on Lena's coat, and when can she pick it up?

Lena 外套上是什么污渍？她什么时候能取件？

查看答案

右袖有红酒渍。她选了普通两天服务，并可以取件时付款。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Drop off for dry cleaning

送去干洗。把衣物交给干洗店时常用。

例句：

I'd like to drop off this coat for dry cleaning.

② Treat a stain

处理污渍。去渍、预处理时常用。

例句：

Can you treat the red wine stain on the sleeve?

③ Same-day pickup

当天取件。加急服务常用说法。

例句：

Is same-day pickup available for this dress?

④ Ready for pickup

可以取件了。店家通知顾客时常用。

例句：

We'll text you when it's ready for pickup.

⑤ Claim ticket

取件单/取衣凭条。取衣服时出示。

例句：

Please bring your claim ticket when you pick it up.

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 dry cleaning 的节奏：DRY CLEANing。注意 stain 的长元音 /steɪn/，pretreat 重音在后：pre-TREAT，以及 claim ticket 的清晰爆破音：CLAIM TICket。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。送洗开场突出 drop OFF，谈污渍时突出 STAIN，取件时突出 CLAIM ticket。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Clothes and cleaning

Do you usually wash clothes at home, or do you use a laundry or dry cleaner?

参考回答（入门训练版）

I wash most clothes at home. For coats and formal clothes, I sometimes use a dry cleaner, especially if there is a stain.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A time you cleaned something carefully

Describe a time you took something to be cleaned. Say what it was, why it needed cleaning, and what happened.

参考回答（入门训练版）

Last month I took my coat to a dry cleaner because of a wine stain. They pretreated it and I picked it up two days later. I was relieved it looked almost new.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Services in daily life

Why do some people prefer professional cleaning services instead of washing everything at home?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Professional cleaners can remove difficult stains and care for delicate fabrics. They also save time for busy people. However, the service costs more than washing at home.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习说明衣服上的污渍、需要几天取件，以及是否当天取件。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意污渍处理、取件时效、短信通知和付款方式。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ①、④、⑥、⑧、⑫ 句的送洗、去渍和取件表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Lena 和 Chris，把污渍类型、取件时间和付款方式换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你会怎么送一件衣服去干洗：污渍、服务时效、价格和取件方式。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

What stain would you ask them to treat?

Would you choose regular or same-day pickup? Why?

Would you pay now or when you pick it up?
