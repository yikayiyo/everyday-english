SPEAKING LESSON · 47

A Job Behind the Curtain

在社区剧场做幕后工作

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

AI 逐词时间轴中个别词可能无高亮，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。零时长词：3 个。

Iris 想参加社区剧场的演出活动，向志愿者 Theo 询问幕后工作并报名一次排练帮忙。 本课属于 2026-11-22 预制系列，练习题为原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Iris · 01

I saw the poster for your community play. Are you looking for help?

我看到了你们社区戏剧的海报。你们需要帮忙吗？

Theo · 02

Yes, we need people backstage as well as actors.

需要，我们既需要演员，也需要幕后工作人员。

Iris · 03

I am shy about acting, but I would like to help.

我不太敢演戏，不过想帮忙。

Theo · 04

We could use someone to organize the props before each scene.

我们需要有人在每场戏之前整理道具。

Iris · 05

What kind of things are the props?

道具都是什么样的东西？

Theo · 06

For this play, they include a suitcase, a hat, and a letter.

这部戏的道具包括一个手提箱、一顶帽子和一封信。

Iris · 07

That sounds manageable. Would someone show me where they go?

听起来我能做。会有人告诉我它们放在哪里吗？

Theo · 08

Yes. Our stage manager has a list and will show you.

会的。我们的舞台监督有清单，会教你。

Iris · 09

I am free Tuesday evening. Could I come to a rehearsal?

我周二晚上有空。我可以来一次排练吗？

Theo · 10

Please do. We start at seven, and I can meet you at the side door.

欢迎。我们七点开始，我可以在侧门接你。

Iris · 11

Wonderful. I will bring a notebook to write things down.

太好了。我会带个笔记本记下来。

Theo · 12

See you Tuesday. We are glad to have your help.

周二见。很高兴有你帮忙。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① backstage

在后台或幕后。

例句：

She helps backstage during the play.

② props

演出道具，是 properties 的简称。

例句：

The table and the letter are props.

③ stage manager

舞台监督，负责协调舞台工作。

例句：

The stage manager checked the list.

④ rehearsal

排练。

例句：

The actors met for a rehearsal.

⑤ write things down

把事情记下来。

例句：

I write things down so I remember them.

03 · American Pronunciation

rehearsal 重音在第二音节 HEAR；props 末尾的 /ps/ 两个辅音都要轻轻带出。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Performances

Do you enjoy watching live performances?

Yes. I like seeing how performers react to the audience, and a small theater can feel very welcoming.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · Helping at an event

Describe an event you helped with. Say what the event was, what you did, and what you learned.

I helped backstage at a small community play last year. The stage manager showed me a list of props, including a hat and a suitcase. Before each scene, I checked that the actors could find what they needed. At first I was nervous about forgetting something, so I wrote notes. The show went smoothly, and I learned that quiet work behind the scenes matters too.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Community arts

Why might a community benefit from a local theater?

It gives people a chance to create something together, including those who prefer working behind the scenes. Performances can also introduce neighbors to stories and ideas they might not otherwise encounter.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

跟读询问幕后工作的句子，再说明你想怎样参与一次演出。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

Are you looking for backstage help? I can organize the props. Could someone show me where everything goes?
