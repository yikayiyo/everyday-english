SPEAKING LESSON · 06

Doing Laundry at a Laundromat

在自助洗衣店洗衣服

A2 → B115–20 分钟家务 · 洗衣店

Lena 第一次来自助洗衣店，向店员 Alex 打听哪台洗衣机空着、用什么洗涤剂，以及烘干机和付款方式。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Lena · 01

Hi! Are any washing machines free right now?

你好！现在有空着的洗衣机吗？

Alex · 02

Yes. Machines four and seven are open. Four is a large load.

有。四号和七号空着。四号是大容量的。

Lena · 03

Great, I'll take four. Do I need to bring my own detergent?

太好了，我用四号。我需要自备洗涤剂吗？

Alex · 04

You can. Or buy a single-use pack from the vending machine by the door.

可以自备。也可以在门口自动售货机买单次用量的包装。

Lena · 05

I'll buy a pack. Which cycle should I use for mixed clothes?

那我买一包。混洗衣物该选哪个程序？

Alex · 06

Use the normal cycle with warm water. It takes about thirty-five minutes.

选标准程序，用温水。大概三十五分钟。

Lena · 07

Got it. And how long do the dryers usually take?

明白了。烘干机一般要多久？

Alex · 08

Most loads need about forty minutes on medium heat.

大多数衣物用中温大约四十分钟就够。

Lena · 09

Can I pay with a card, or do I need coins?

我能刷卡吗，还是必须用硬币？

Alex · 10

Either works. You can tap your card, use the app, or add coins.

都行。可以刷卡、用 App，也可以投币。

Lena · 11

Perfect. Should I stay here the whole time, or can I come back later?

太好了。我要一直待在这儿，还是可以过会儿再回来？

Alex · 12

You can step out. Just come back before the cycle ends so nobody moves your stuff.

可以出去。只要在程序结束前回来，免得别人挪走你的衣服。

听力理解

不看中文，再听一遍对话，尝试回答：

Which machine does Lena choose, and how can she pay?

Lena 选了哪台机器？她可以怎么付款？

查看答案

她选了四号大容量洗衣机。付款可以刷卡、用 App，也可以投币。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Are any … free right now?

现在有……空着吗？询问是否有空位或空闲设备。

例句：

Are any dryers free right now?

② I'll take …

我选……/我要……，用于选定某台机器或某样东西。

例句：

I'll take machine seven.

③ Which cycle should I use…?

我该选哪个洗衣程序？询问机器设置。

例句：

Which cycle should I use for towels?

④ Either works.

两种都可以。用于给出灵活选择。

例句：

Card or coins? Either works.

⑤ Come back before the cycle ends

在程序结束前回来。提醒时间安排。

例句：

Come back before the cycle ends so your clothes stay safe.

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 laundromat /ˈlɔːndrəmæt/ 的重音在 LAUN。注意 detergent 的重音在第二音节 -TER-，以及 thirty-five minutes 的连读。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。询问空机器时突出 Are any … FREE?，谈程序时突出 normal CYCLE 和 THIRTY-FIVE minutes。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Household chores

Do you usually do your own laundry?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Yes, I do. I wash my clothes once or twice a week. Sometimes I use a laundromat when I have a big load.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A place you go regularly

Describe a place you go to do everyday tasks. Say where it is, what you do there, and why you go there.

参考回答（入门训练版）

I sometimes go to a laundromat near my apartment. It has large washing machines and dryers. I go there when I have too many clothes for my small machine at home.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Shared facilities

Why do some people prefer shared laundry facilities instead of washing at home?

参考回答（入门训练版）

I think shared facilities can save space and money, especially in small apartments. They also have larger machines, so big loads finish faster. Some people like that they don't need to buy a washer themselves.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习询问空洗衣机、选择程序和付款方式。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意空机器、洗涤剂、洗衣程序、烘干时间和付款方式。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ①、③、⑤、⑨、⑪ 句的询问、选择程序和付款表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Lena 和 Alex，把洗衣量、程序和付款方式换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你如何找空机器、选洗涤程序，以及你更喜欢刷卡还是投币。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

How would you ask if a washing machine is free?

Which cycle would you choose, and why?

Would you pay by card, app, or coins? Why?
