SPEAKING LESSON · 12

Checking in at the Airport

在机场办理登机

A2 → B115–20 分钟机场 · 登机

Nina 要飞往芝加哥，来到航空公司柜台办理登机。她向工作人员 Cole 出示护照和订票确认，托运一个行李箱并随身带背包，选择靠窗座位，并询问安检位置、登机时间和登机口。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Nina · 01

Hi. I'd like to check in for the flight to Chicago, please.

你好。我想办理飞往芝加哥的航班登机手续。

Cole · 02

Of course. May I see your passport and booking confirmation?

当然可以。我可以看一下你的护照和订票确认吗？

Nina · 03

Sure. Here they are. I'm on the three-forty flight.

好的。都在这里。我订的是三点四十的航班。

Cole · 04

Thank you. How many bags are you checking today?

谢谢。今天要托运几个行李？

Nina · 05

Just one suitcase. I'd also like to carry on this backpack.

就一个行李箱。这个背包我想随身携带。

Cole · 06

That's fine. Would you prefer a window seat or an aisle seat?

没问题。你想要靠窗的座位，还是靠过道的座位？

Nina · 07

A window seat, please, if one is still available.

请给我靠窗的座位，如果还有的话。

Cole · 08

I can give you a window seat near the front. Your bag is just under the weight limit.

我可以给你一个靠近前排的靠窗座位。你的行李刚好在重量限制以内。

Nina · 09

Great. Do I need to go through security right away?

太好了。我需要马上过安检吗？

Cole · 10

Yes. Security is down the hall to your left. Boarding starts at three o'clock.

是的。安检就在走廊左边。三点开始登机。

Nina · 11

Okay. Where can I find the gate number?

好的。我在哪里可以看到登机口号码？

Cole · 12

It's printed on your boarding pass. You're at Gate B fourteen. Have a good flight!

印在你的登机牌上。你在 B 十四号登机口。祝你飞行愉快！

听力理解

不看中文，再听一遍对话，尝试回答：

What does Nina take on the flight, which seat does she get, and where does she board?

Nina 带了什么行李，坐什么座位，在哪个登机口登机？

查看答案

她托运一个行李箱，并随身携带背包。她得到靠近前排的靠窗座位，行李刚好在重量限制以内。安检在走廊左边，三点开始登机。登机口是 B 十四号，印在登机牌上。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① Check in for the flight

办理航班登机手续。在机场柜台说明来意时常用。

例句：

I'd like to check in for the flight to Chicago, please.

② Booking confirmation

订票确认。办理登机时通常要出示护照和订票确认。

例句：

May I see your passport and booking confirmation?

③ Check a bag / carry on

托运行李 / 随身携带。说明哪些行李托运、哪些带上飞机时常用。

例句：

Just one suitcase. I'd also like to carry on this backpack.

④ A window seat or an aisle seat

靠窗座位还是靠过道座位。选择座位时常用。注意 aisle 读作 /aɪl/。

例句：

Would you prefer a window seat or an aisle seat?

⑤ Boarding pass

登机牌。上面有航班、座位和登机口等信息。

例句：

It's printed on your boarding pass. You're at Gate B fourteen.

03 · American Pronunciation

练习 aisle 的发音：它读作 I'll，/aɪl/，中间的 s 不发音。注意 check in 的节奏：CHECK in，boarding pass 的重音在前：BOAR-ding pass，以及 confirmation 的重音在第三音节：con-fir-MA-tion。

先慢读，再跟随音频模仿节奏。说过道座位时把 aisle 读成 I'll，说登机牌时突出 BOAR-ding pass，说订票确认时突出 con-fir-MA-tion。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · Air travel

Do you like traveling by plane? Why or why not?

参考回答（入门训练版）

I quite like traveling by plane because it is fast, and I can get to another city in a few hours. I don't like the long lines at security, but a window seat makes the flight more enjoyable.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A time you were at an airport

Describe a time you were at an airport. Say where you were going, what you did at check-in, and how you felt.

参考回答（入门训练版）

Last summer I went to the airport to fly to Chicago. At check-in I showed my passport, checked one suitcase, and asked for a window seat. The agent gave me a boarding pass for Gate B fourteen, and I went through security early. I felt a little nervous, but everything went smoothly.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · Travel and airports

Why do some people prefer to check in online instead of at the airport counter?

参考回答（入门训练版）

Online check-in can save time, especially when the airport is busy. People can choose a seat earlier and go straight to the bag drop or security. Some travelers still use the counter if they have questions, extra bags, or a problem with their booking.

07 · Shadowing Practice 跟读训练

先听完整对话，再逐句跟读。最后练习说明你要去哪里、托运什么行李、想坐什么座位，以及如何找到登机口。

第一轮：Listen & Understand（3 分钟）

听完整对话，留意目的地、行李、座位选择、安检和登机口。

第二轮：Listen & Repeat（5 分钟）

逐句暂停跟读，重点练习第 ①、②、⑤、⑥、⑫ 句的办理登机、订票确认、托运与随身行李、座位和登机牌表达。

第三轮：Role-play（5 分钟）

分别扮演 Nina 和 Cole，把目的地、行李和座位换成自己的情况来练习。

08 · 今日口头回答练习

用 3–4 句说明你会怎么在机场办理登机：目的地、护照和订票确认、行李和座位，以及登机牌上的登机口。

可以用这些问题帮助组织回答：

Where are you flying, and what time is your flight?

What bags will you check, and what will you carry on?

Would you like a window seat or an aisle seat?

Where can you find the gate number?
