Helping at a School Reading Day

帮忙筹备学校阅读日

A2 → B115–20 分钟预制 · 日常口语

预制系列 · 2026-11-22；练习题为原创，非官方真题。

语音与逐词时间轴由 AI 生成，尚未人工逐句试听与校对。

家长 Emily 和老师 Ben 商量阅读日的志愿工作与接送信息。 本课为 2026-11-22 预制系列原创练习，并非真实考试题。

01 · 场景对话

FULL DIALOGUE

听完整对话

12 句连续播放，适合通勤或跟读前先过一遍。

Emily · 01

I saw the note about reading day. Could I help at the event?

我看到阅读日的通知了。我能在活动中帮忙吗？

Ben · 02

We would appreciate that. Can you help children find their reading groups?

非常感谢。你能帮孩子们找到各自的阅读小组吗？

Emily · 03

Yes. Where should I meet them?

可以。我在哪里接他们？

Ben · 04

Please meet them at the classroom door after lunch.

请午饭后在教室门口接他们。

Emily · 05

Will there be a list of the groups?

会有分组名单吗？

Ben · 06

Yes, I will give you one with the room names.

会的，我会给你一份写有教室名称的名单。

Emily · 07

My son is in your class. May I bring him home afterward?

我儿子在您的班上。活动结束后我可以带他回家吗？

Ben · 08

Yes. Please let me know before you leave with him.

可以。带他离开前请告诉我一声。

Emily · 09

Of course. Do you need me to bring any books?

当然。需要我带书吗？

Ben · 10

No, the school has prepared enough for this event.

不用，学校为这次活动准备了足够的书。

Emily · 11

Then I will come a little early to look at the list.

那我会早点来看看名单。

Ben · 12

Thank you, Emily. I will see you at the classroom door.

谢谢你，Emily。教室门口见。

02 · Useful Expressions 地道表达

① help at an event

在活动中帮忙。

例句：

My sister helped at the school concert.

② reading group

阅读小组。

例句：

Our reading group meets on Friday.

③ afterward

之后，指前面提到的事情结束后。

例句：

We watched a play and talked afterward.

④ let me know

告诉我，让我知道。

例句：

Please let me know when you arrive.

⑤ a little early

稍微早一点。

例句：

I arrived a little early to find my seat.

03 · American Pronunciation

classroom 重音在第一音节；在 let me 中，两个词连贯但仍保留 /t/ 的收尾。

04 · IELTS Speaking Part 1

Part 1 · School events

Did you enjoy school events as a child?

Yes. I liked reading days because we could listen to stories with children from other classes.

05 · IELTS Speaking Part 2

Part 2 · A school activity

Describe a school activity you helped with or attended. Say what happened, who was there, and how you felt.

I helped at a reading day at my son's school. After lunch, I met a group of children at their classroom door and helped them find the right room. The teacher gave me a list, so I could check where everyone needed to go. I enjoyed listening to the children talk about their books. At the end, I told the teacher before taking my son home.

这是入门短答，请补充个人经历并扩展到接近两分钟。

06 · IELTS Speaking Part 3

Part 3 · School and families

How can schools make it easier for families to join events?

Schools can give clear information in advance and offer different ways to help. Short tasks and accessible times may allow more families to take part.

跟读询问志愿任务的对话，再确认地点和需要携带的物品。
